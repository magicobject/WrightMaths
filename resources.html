Lessons

In person around Leominster, or live online — your choice.

Every student gets a lesson format that suits them: face-to-face at a convenient local location, or online with a shared digital whiteboard and screen-share. Same quality of teaching either way.

In person

Around Leominster & North Herefordshire

Face-to-face lessons in or around Leominster, with travel possible to Kington, Ludlow, Hereford, Bromyard and Presteigne by arrangement. A parent is always welcome to be present, especially for a first session.

Online

Anywhere in the UK

Live one-to-one lessons via video call with a shared digital whiteboard, so working is visible step-by-step just as it would be on paper. Materials and past papers shared securely afterwards.

What to expect

How lessons work

1. Free first chat

A short call to understand where the student is now, what board and level, and what "success" looks like.

2. Baseline assessment

A light-touch review of recent work or past-paper questions to spot the real priority topics.

3. Focused lessons

Typically weekly, hour-long sessions building steadily toward exams, with past-paper practice ramping up nearer the date.

Rates depend on level and format — get in touch for current pricing and availability.
