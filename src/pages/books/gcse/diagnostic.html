Find your gaps

Where should I start?

Eleven quick questions, about ten minutes. It is not a test. You get a map of your gaps and your first three topics to try.

GCSE Maths Book / Find your gaps

How it works: answer what you can. Leave a box empty if you do not know. No hints and no calculator. Where the answer is a fraction, type it like 1/4.

At the end you get a map of your gaps and your first three topics to try. Come back in a month to see how far you have come.

Your answers are checked in this browser and never sent anywhere. A half-finished check is kept only until you close the tab. Your finished results are saved only in this browser, on this device.
