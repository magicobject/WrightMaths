Question words

What the question is asking you to do

Exam questions start with a small word that tells you what to write. Learn these words and you will lose fewer marks.

GCSE Maths Book / Question words

Key idea: the number of marks tells you how much to write. One mark is a short answer. Three or four marks need working. Always show your working when there are two or more marks.

The words, one by one

{term:work-out}

Do: do the calculation and give the answer.

Example: Work out 3 + 4 × 2. Write 4 × 2 = 8, then 3 + 8 = 11.

{term:calculate}

Do: the same as work out. You may use a calculator. Show your working.

Example: Calculate 15% of 80. Write 0.15 × 80 = 12.

{term:write-down}

Do: give the answer only. No working is needed, so do not spend time on it.

Example: Write down the next number: 2, 4, 6, 8. Write 10.

Find

Do: get the missing number, length or angle. Show how you got it.

Example: Find the missing side. Use Pythagoras, then write the answer with its unit.

{term:show-that}

Do: the answer is given. Write every step that leads to it. Do not just write the answer.

Example: Show that x = 4 is a solution of 3x − 1 = 11.

Write 3 × 4 − 1 = 11, then finish with a sentence: “so x = 4 is a solution”. The closing sentence earns a mark.

{term:explain-command|Explain} or give a reason

Do: write a sentence with because. Use the right maths word, such as “alternate angles are equal”. The letters Z, F and C are not accepted as reasons: write the name.

Example: Explain why 1 is not prime. It has only one factor, and a prime needs exactly two.

{term:estimate|Estimate}

Do: round each number to something easy and work out a rough answer. A calculator answer is not wanted.

Example: Estimate 48.7 × 19.8. Round each number to 1 significant figure: 50 × 20 = 1,000.

Simplify

Do: make it shorter or neater: collect like terms, or write a fraction in its lowest terms.

Example: Simplify 5x + 2 − x + 4. Write 4x + 6.

Solve

Do: find the value of the unknown that makes the equation true. Check it by putting it back.

Example: Solve 3x + 5 = 20. Write x = 5.

Expand and factorise

Do: expand removes brackets. Factorise puts brackets back. They undo each other.

Example: Expand 3(x + 4) to get 3x + 12.

Factorise 3x + 12 to get 3(x + 4).

The two are always equal: we write 3(x + 4) {term:identity|≡} 3x + 12.

{term:hence}

Do: use the answer you have just found to get the next one. Do not start again.

Example: You factorised x² + 5x + 6 as (x + 2)(x + 3). Hence solve x² + 5x + 6 = 0. Write x = −2 or x = −3.

{term:deduce}

Do: use a fact you are given to get a new fact, and say which fact you used.

Example: 23 × 14 = 322. Deduce 2.3 × 14. Write: 2.3 is 23 ÷ 10, so 322 ÷ 10 = 32.2.

{term:justify}

Do: give a reason that shows your answer is right. Yes or no on its own earns nothing.

Example: Is 51 prime? Justify your answer. Write: no, because 3 × 17 = 51.

{term:evaluate}

Do: put the numbers in and work out the value.

Example: Evaluate 3a + 2 when a = 4. Write 3 × 4 + 2 = 14.

{term:sketch}

Do: draw a rough picture with the right shape. Label the points the question asks for. No ruler is needed.

Example: Sketch y = x² − 4. Draw a U-shape that crosses the x-axis at −2 and 2.

{term:prove}

Do: show, with clear steps, that something is always true. Use letters, not just one example.

Example: Prove that two odd numbers add to an even number. Write 2m + 1 and 2n + 1. Their sum is 2(m + n + 1), which is even.

{term:describe}

Do: say in words what you see or what happens, with the key numbers.

Example: Describe the pattern 3, 7, 11, 15. Write: it goes up by 4 each time.

Check your knowledge: question words

The question says Write down the next number in 2, 4, 6, 8. What should you do?

  • Write the answer only, with no working
  • Write a long explanation with every step
  • Use a calculator and show every key you pressed
  1. Write down means give the answer only.

The answer is 10. Write 10 and move on.

The question says Show that the answer is 12. What do the marks reward?

  • Writing 12 as the final answer
  • Every step of working that leads to 12
  • Finding a different answer to check
  1. The answer is given. What is left for you to give?

The marks are for the route, not the destination.

You are asked to explain why the angles in a triangle cannot be 60°, 70° and 40°. Which answer earns the mark?

  • The angles are wrong.
  • They add up to 170°, but the angles in a triangle must add up to 180°.
  • All the angles must be 90°.
  1. An explanation uses because: give the fact and the numbers.

60 + 70 + 40 = 170, which is not 180.

The question says Estimate 48.7 × 19.8. What do you write?

  • 964.26
  • 50 × 20 = 1,000
  • 49 + 20 = 69
  1. Round each number to 1 significant figure first.

48.7 is about 50 and 19.8 is about 20, so 50 × 20 = 1,000.
