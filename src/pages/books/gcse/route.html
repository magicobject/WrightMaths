Which route is for you?

The pyramid shows how topics fit together. A route turns it into a week-by-week plan. Pick the one that fits, and change it whenever you like.

Where you will use this: a resit with a deadline, a term of revision, or a plan a parent or teacher can check.

GCSE Maths Book / Study routes

How to use a route: do about three 20-minute sessions a week. In a session, try 10 to 12 questions from one topic: you do not have to finish every set. If a topic feels easy, press “Skip the basics” at the top of its page. If it feels hard, go back to the topic it builds on. A topic marked Higher is only needed for Higher tier papers.
