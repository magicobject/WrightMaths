Changelog

Every build, newest first

  • 2026.11.25.01025 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book (still unlinked): the typed gaps in “Now you finish it” boxes now cover every chapter that has them (about 300 gaps). A few boxes whose answers cannot be typed (a symbol such as <, a reading off a graph, an answer inside a fraction) keep the show-me dropdown. Two gaps in one step that take the same pair of numbers (such as 5 + 9) must use each number once, and a bracket printed beside a gap may be typed. Every answer key was checked by a second agent.

  • 2026.11.25.00925 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book (still unlinked), pilot: the gaps (___) in “Now you finish it” boxes are now small typed boxes with one Check button. Right gaps turn green and lock, wrong ones are marked red without giving the answer away, and the worked answer opens once every gap is right. Done for Fractions and Averages (about 30 gaps); the other chapters still use the show-me dropdown until the pilot is approved.

  • 2026.11.25.00825 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book, answer checker: where a question asks for several answers written like “1 or 5” (the roots of a quadratic, for example in books/gcse/functions-graphs.html), the checker now also accepts them in either order and with the usual separators: “1 5”, “1, 5”, “5 and 1”. A coordinate pair such as (4, −4) still has to be in order.

  • 2026.11.25.00625 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book, Listen voice rules: function notation is now read as words (“f(x) = 5x − 3. Work out f(4)” becomes “f of x equals five x minus three. Work out f of four”), and the recording script now leaves an extra gap after every full stop, never after a decimal point (decimals are spoken as “point” words). Sentences with function notation need new recordings, and every clip needs re-making to get the longer gaps (python scripts/piper-batch.py --redo); Piper is not installed yet, so no clips have changed.

  • 2026.11.25.00525 November 2026

    Home page: the “Degree-qualified & PGCE-trained” badge now says “Qualified Teacher Status (QTS)” in place of “formal teacher training”. Page touched: home.

  • 2026.11.25.00425 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book, averages (books/gcse/averages.html) and the median glossary entry: “in order” now says “in order from smallest to largest” wherever the median is explained or hinted. Sentences changed, so their recordings need re-making.

  • 2026.11.25.00325 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book, glossary (books/gcse/glossary.html): technical words inside an entry now link to their own entries (Rationalising the denominator links “surd”, and so on; once per entry, never to itself), checked by a new unit test. Reflection has a diagram (a grid showing (1, 2) reflected in x = 3 and in y = x) and says “Reflect the point”; the Translation and Rotation examples say “the point” too; the relative frequency example says “a dropped drawing pin”.
