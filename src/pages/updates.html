An internal changelog: every build number and what changed. This page isn't linked from anywhere else on the site.

Changelog

Every build, newest first

  • 2026.11.24.00524 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book (still unlinked): phone and quiet-help fixes from round 3. Number answers open the number pad, and a symbol row types minus, fraction bar, point and percent. The Check button stays in view above the keyboard, and buttons are at least 44px tall. “Help, I’m stuck!” is now “Give me a clue”, the duck step is “Say it out loud” with typing tucked away, the last step reads “Show a mate, or take a photo” with no mention of lessons, and Give me five says so if the questions cannot be loaded.

  • 2026.11.24.00424 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book (still unlinked): hints are now lettered Hint A, Hint B, Hint C with more space, so a hint that starts with a number (such as 2 + (−5)) is never mistaken for a list number. After a right answer the hints start again from Hint A.

  • 2026.11.24.00124 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book (still unlinked): round 2 of testing. About 160 question diagrams, hover definitions with pictures for technical words in questions, a “Find your gaps” check (books/gcse/diagnostic.html), a spaced “ready to revisit” queue for Give me five, Help still available after answering, a more natural on-device Listen voice with voice and speed choices, the identity sign in the glossary, recap questions folded away, faded answers hidden until asked for, sport, music and art contexts, many more wrong-answer messages, and precision fixes to proofs and definitions. Privacy and access fixes from expert review: book pages no longer load Google Fonts, Listen never uses an online voice unless chosen, a skip link, named questions and answer boxes, keyboard access to term pop-ups, and a limit on answer length.

  • 2026.11.23.00723 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book (still unlinked): display fixes from the UX review. The header no longer sticks on book pages, long pages have an “On this page” list and a Top button (headings now carry stable ids), reading options have 44px buttons, a clear on/off state and a size indicator, the reading ruler is a see-through highlight, feedback has distinct tick, nearly and cross icons, the BIDMAS staircase no longer implies a ranking inside each pair, the scale diagram is tidier, the hub shows the pyramid first, and a temperature typed with its unit (for example 5°C) is accepted.

  • 2026.11.23.00623 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book (still unlinked): first round of student feedback applied. New pages for mixed practice (books/gcse/practice.html) and question words (books/gcse/command-words.html); reading options, Listen buttons, a lighter duck, amber “nearly” feedback, generic hints for sign and decimal-point slips, shuffled multiple choice, a “Words you need” strip on every topic, new pictures, proof and 3D Pythagoras, surds, double brackets, more exam practice, and four wrong keyed answers fixed with a new test that stops that class of bug.

  • 2026.11.23.00523 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book (still unlinked): added the rubber duck to the “Help, I’m stuck!” ladder. After the hints, students are asked to explain the problem to a duck (what is it asking, what do I know, what did I try, where did it stop making sense) before being pointed to a person. What they type stays on screen and is never stored or sent. Added an eighth habit to the raise-your-grade guide (books/gcse/raise-your-grade.html).

  • 2026.11.23.00423 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book (still unlinked): finished the base layer with Order of operations (books/gcse/order-of-operations.html), Factors and multiples (books/gcse/factors-multiples.html), Squares and square roots (books/gcse/squares-roots.html), Shapes and angles (books/gcse/shapes-angles.html) and Algebra basics (books/gcse/algebra-basics.html), about 210 more questions and 18 new glossary terms. Equations are now explained with a traditional kitchen scale diagram in Algebra basics and Solving equations: the right value for the unknown is what makes both sides balance.

  • 2026.11.23.00323 November 2026

    Hidden GCSE book (still unlinked): wrote the first three base-layer topics the pilot stands on, Place value (books/gcse/place-value.html), Add, subtract, multiply, divide (books/gcse/four-operations.html) and Negative numbers (books/gcse/negative-numbers.html), about 130 more check-your-knowledge questions, and ten new glossary terms. A topic with nothing underneath it now says so in its "Before you start" box.

  • 2026.11.23.00223 November 2026

    Fixed the previous entry: it linked to the hidden GCSE book pages, which the tests rightly refuse. The paths are now plain text, so nothing on the site links to the book.

  • 2026.11.23.00123 November 2026

    Started a hidden pilot of a GCSE maths textbook at books/gcse/: three topics (Fractions (books/gcse/fractions.html), Solving equations (books/gcse/solving-equations.html), Pythagoras (books/gcse/pythagoras.html)) with about 100 check-your-knowledge questions, a pyramid of what each topic builds on, a glossary (books/gcse/glossary.html) of maths terms with examples, and a guide to raising your grade (books/gcse/raise-your-grade.html). Not linked from anywhere and marked noindex. The page builder now supports pages in folders, and an axe-core accessibility test runs on every page (the main site's two known colour-contrast issues are noted in the test).
