How a lesson works

Diagnose, then teach to the gap

  • We start with a short assessment so the first proper lesson targets real gaps, not assumed ones.
  • Lessons follow the specification and mark scheme for your exact exam board — AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC or IB.
  • Between sessions you get a clear note of what was covered and what to practise, so progress compounds rather than resets each week.
