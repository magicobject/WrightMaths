Free resources

The best free maths resources, by level.

A hand-picked list I actually recommend to my students — free to use whether or not you tutor with me. Bookmark this page.

GCSE

  • Corbettmaths

    Video tutorials, practice questions and exam-style "5-a-day" for every GCSE topic.

  • Maths Genie

    Topic-by-topic worksheets, past papers and model answers by grade.

  • Dr Frost Maths

    Free, well-sequenced practice with automatic marking — excellent for independent revision.

  • BBC Bitesize — GCSE Maths

    Clear topic summaries and quizzes, good for a quick refresher.

A Level ★ speciality

  • Physics & Maths Tutor (PMT)

    The single best source for A Level past papers, topic questions and mark schemes across all boards.

  • Maths Genie — A Level

    Pure, statistics and mechanics topic tests with worked solutions.

  • ExamSolutions

    Step-by-step video walkthroughs, particularly strong for pure maths methods.

  • Integral Mathematics (MEI)

    Structured online resources for A Level and Further Maths (school/college login usually required).

  • Underground Mathematics

    Rich, University of Cambridge–built resources that build real understanding beyond rote method.

IB Diploma ★ speciality

  • Revision Village

    The most complete IB-specific question bank and video course for AA and AI, SL and HL.

  • Exam-Mate

    Searchable past-paper questions by topic for IB Maths — very useful nearer exams.

  • Haese Mathematics

    Publisher of the standard IB Maths textbooks, with sample chapters and worked examples.

Beyond A Level / IB — university & lifelong learning ★ speciality

  • STEP Support (Cambridge)

    Official support programme for STEP, the entrance exam for Cambridge and other top maths courses.

  • MAT / TMUA practice (PMT)

    Past papers and worked solutions for the Oxford MAT and TMUA admissions tests.

  • MIT OpenCourseWare — Mathematics

    Full free university-level courses, lecture notes and problem sets from MIT.

  • 3Blue1Brown

    Beautifully visual explanations of calculus, linear algebra and more — brilliant for real intuition.

  • Brilliant

    Interactive courses in maths and quantitative reasoning for curious adults and advanced students.

  • Khan Academy — Maths

    Free structured courses from arithmetic right through to multivariable calculus.
