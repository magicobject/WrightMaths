    How to use this book

    A short guide to how each page is laid out, how to get help on a question, and where your progress is kept.

    A-level Maths Book / How to use this book

    What the book covers

    This book covers A-level pure maths. The content is the same for every English exam board (AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR A and OCR MEI), because they all teach the same government-set subject content. Where one board asks for something the others do not, the page says so. Mechanics and statistics are not in this book.

    Year 1 is the AS content and the first year of A-level. Year 2 is the A-level-only content. The Year 2 book is coming soon.

    How a topic page is laid out

    1. Words you need. The terms the topic uses, each in one plain sentence.
    2. Last time. A few questions on what the topic builds on.
    3. The lesson. Each idea has a diagram, a worked example, and then an example where you finish the working.
    4. Check your knowledge. Questions after each part of the lesson.
    5. Flip it. The same idea worked in reverse. Doing both ways helps you remember it.
    6. Exam style, common traps and an "I can" list.

    Getting help on a question

    Every question has a "Help, I'm stuck!" button. It gives one clue at a time, from "what is the question asking?" to the first step. Try the question first. The full worked solution is the last thing to open.

    How the maths is written

    Maths is written as ordinary text on the page, so you can zoom in, change the text size and use a screen reader. When you type an answer, write it on one line: for example x^2+5x+4 for x squared plus 5x plus 4, and (x+1)/(x-2) for a fraction.

    The book uses sin−1 for the inverse sine, as on a calculator. Every exam board also accepts arcsin, and the glossary lists both.

    Your progress and your privacy

    Your ticks and answers are saved in your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere: there are no accounts, no tracking and no outside services. If you use a different device or clear your browser's data, your progress starts again.

    This book has no voice

    The A-level book does not read pages aloud. A screen reader works on every page. The text-size, easy-read font, tinted page and reading-ruler options are under "Reading options" at the top of each page.
