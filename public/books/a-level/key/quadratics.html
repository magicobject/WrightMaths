    Working: Many pairs of numbers multiply to 10. Only 0 forces a factor to be 0. Subtract 10 from both sides first: x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0, so (x - 5)(x + 2) = 0 and x = 5 or x = -2. Check x = 10: 100 - 30 = 70, not 10.

    Working: (-3)^2 = 9 as well as 3^2 = 9. So x = 3 or x = -3. Factorising shows it: (x - 3)(x + 3) = 0.
    64Tom says: x^2 + 6x + 1 = (x + 3)^2 + 1. What went wrong?He did not subtract 9. The correct form is (x + 3)^2 - 8
    Working: (x + 3)^2 + 1 = x^2 + 6x + 10, not x^2 + 6x + 1. Subtract the extra 9: x^2 + 6x + 1 = (x + 3)^2 - 9 + 1 = (x + 3)^2 - 8.
