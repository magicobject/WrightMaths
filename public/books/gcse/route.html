    Study routes

    Which route is for you?

    The pyramid shows how topics fit together. A route turns it into a week-by-week plan. Pick the route that fits, and change it whenever you like.

    Use a route for a resit with a deadline, a term of revision, or a plan a parent or teacher can check.

    Find my gaps first Back to the pyramid
    GCSE Maths Book / Study routes

    How to use a route: do about three 20-minute sessions a week. In a session, try 10 to 12 questions from one topic: you do not have to finish every set. If a topic feels easy, press “Skip the basics” at the top of its page. If it feels hard, go back to the topic it builds on. A topic marked Higher is only needed for Higher tier papers.

    Resit to a grade 4 (8 weeks)

    For anyone who needs a grade 4 and has a fixed date. Foundation topics only, in the order that works best. Do the Find my gaps check first and start where it points.

    1. Week 1: Place value, Add, subtract, multiply, divide
    2. Week 2: Negative numbers, Order of operations
    3. Week 3: Fractions, Factors and multiples
    4. Week 4: Percentages, Ratio
    5. Week 5: Algebra basics, Solving equations
    6. Week 6: Shapes and angles, Area and perimeter, Pythagoras' theorem
    7. Week 7: Averages and spread, Probability, Sequences
    8. Week 8: Practice papers, grades 4 to 6

    The whole book (12 weeks)

    Four topics a week, base first. Each topic has about 50 to 100 questions: you do not have to finish every set. Use "Skip the basics" if a topic feels easy.

    1. Week 1: Place value, Add, subtract, multiply, divide, Negative numbers, Order of operations
    2. Week 2: Factors and multiples, Squares and square roots, Fractions, Algebra basics
    3. Week 3: Shapes and angles, Area and perimeter, Percentages, Ratio
    4. Week 4: Solving equations, Powers and indices, Probability, Sequences
    5. Week 5: Coordinates and straight-line graphs, Pythagoras' theorem, Averages and spread, Circles
    6. Week 6: Direct and inverse proportion, Repeated percentage change Higher, Inequalities, Rearranging formulae
    7. Week 7: Simultaneous equations, Quadratic equations Higher, Similar shapes, Transformations
    8. Week 8: Surds Higher, Trigonometry, Bearings, Speed, density and other compound measures
    9. Week 9: Histograms, cumulative frequency and box plots Higher, Conditional probability Higher, Constructions and loci, Circle theorems Higher
    10. Week 10: Bounds and error intervals Higher, Vectors Higher, Algebraic fractions Higher, Functions and graphs Higher
    11. Week 11: Sine and cosine rules Higher, Iteration Higher, Practice papers, grades 4 to 6
    12. Week 12: Grade 7 to 9 stretch papers

    Two or three weak topics only (3 weeks)

    For a student who is wobbling on algebra and ratio and is fine with the rest. Three short blocks: algebra first, then ratio, then percentages, which uses both. Do about three 20-minute sessions on each. Stop and move on if the "Check your knowledge" questions at the end of a topic come out right.

    1. Week 1: Algebra basics
    2. Week 2: Ratio
    3. Week 3: Percentages

    Grade 7 to 9 top-up (6 weeks)

    For students already secure at grade 6 who want the Higher material. Skip any topic you can already do.

    1. Week 1: Surds Higher, Powers and indices, Bounds and error intervals Higher
    2. Week 2: Quadratic equations Higher, Functions and graphs Higher, Iteration Higher
    3. Week 3: Sine and cosine rules Higher, Circle theorems Higher, Vectors Higher
    4. Week 4: Conditional probability Higher, Histograms, cumulative frequency and box plots Higher, Algebraic fractions Higher
    5. Week 5: Repeated percentage change Higher, Direct and inverse proportion, Similar shapes
    6. Week 6: Grade 7 to 9 stretch papers
