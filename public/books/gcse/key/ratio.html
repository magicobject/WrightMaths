    Ratio: answers

    74 questions, 7 of them marked (22 marks in all). Question numbers match the page. Use your browser's Print for a copy.

    Answers for Ratio
    No.QuestionAnswerMarks
    1What fraction of the counters are red? Give your answer as a fraction, like 2/3.3/5
    Working: 12 red out of 12 + 8 = 20 counters. 12/20 = 3/5.
    2Simplify 12/30 fully. Give your answer as a fraction, like 3/4.2/5
    Working: The HCF of 12 and 30 is 6. 12 ÷ 6 = 2 and 30 ÷ 6 = 5, so 2/5.
    3What is 3/5 of 40?24
    Working: 40 ÷ 5 = 8, and 3 × 8 = 24.
    4Cement and sand are mixed in the ratio 2 : 6. Write this ratio in its simplest form, like 2:5.1:3
    Working: 2 ÷ 2 = 1 and 6 ÷ 2 = 3, so the ratio is 1:3. Check: 1 : 3 = 2 : 6 ✓
    5A pancake recipe uses 250 g of flour and 100 g of sugar. Write the ratio of flour to sugar in its simplest form, like 3:4.5:2
    Working: The HCF of 250 and 100 is 50. 250 ÷ 50 = 5 and 100 ÷ 50 = 2, so flour : sugar = 5:2.
    6Which ratio is equivalent to 3 : 4?6 : 8
    Working: 3 × 2 = 6 and 4 × 2 = 8, so 6 : 8 is equivalent to 3 : 4.
    7Fill the gap: 3 : 5 = 12 : ?. What number goes where the ? is?20
    Working: 3 × 4 = 12, so multiply 5 by 4 as well: 5 × 4 = 20. So 3 : 5 = 12 : 20.
    8A plank is 40 cm long and a pipe is 2 m long. Write the ratio of the plank to the pipe in its simplest form, like 1:2.1:5
    Working: 2 m = 200 cm. 40 : 200, divide both sides by 40, gives 1:5.
    9Ravi has won 18 matches and lost 12 matches on his console game. Write the ratio of wins to losses in its simplest form, like 3:4. [Figure: Bar model for sharing 60 in the ratio 2 to 3]3:2
    Working: The HCF of 18 and 12 is 6. 18 ÷ 6 = 3 and 12 ÷ 6 = 2, so wins : losses = 3:2.
    10Share £40 in the ratio 1 : 3. How much is the smaller share, in pounds? [Figure: Bar model for sharing 40 in the ratio 1 to 3]10
    Working: 1 + 3 = 4 parts. One part is £40 ÷ 4 = £10. The smaller share is 1 × 10 = £10. (The larger is £30.)
    11Mia and Tom share a £72 prize in the ratio 4 : 5. How much does Tom get, in pounds? [Figure: Bar model for the prize]40
    Working: 4 + 5 = 9 parts. One part is £72 ÷ 9 = £8. Tom gets 5 × 8 = £40. (Mia gets £32, and 32 + 40 = 72 ✓.)
    12A bricklayer mixes cement, sand and gravel in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3 to make 90 kg of mix. How many kilograms of sand are in it? [Figure: Bar model for the mix]30
    Working: 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 parts. One part is 90 ÷ 6 = 15 kg. Sand is 2 × 15 = 30 kg. (Cement 15 kg, gravel 45 kg: 15 + 30 + 45 = 90 ✓.)
    13A band shares £350 from a gig between the guitarist, the drummer and the singer in the ratio 2 : 2 : 3. How much does the singer get, in pounds?150
    Working: 2 + 2 + 3 = 7 parts. One part is £350 ÷ 7 = £50. The singer gets 3 × 50 = £150. (The others get £100 each, and 100 + 100 + 150 = 350 ✓.)
    14Share 30 free sample sachets in the ratio 2 : 3. Which pair of shares is right?12 and 18
    Working: 2 + 3 = 5 parts, so one part is 30 ÷ 5 = 6. The shares are 2 × 6 = 12 and 3 × 6 = 18.
    15A plumber mixes cleaning concentrate with water in the ratio 1 : 4 to make 35 litres of solution. How many litres of concentrate does she need?7
    Working: 1 + 4 = 5 parts. One part is 35 ÷ 5 = 7 litres of concentrate. (Water is 28 litres, and 7 + 28 = 35 ✓.)
    16The ratio of cats to dogs on a street is 3 : 7. What fraction of the pets are dogs? Give your answer as a fraction, like 3/10.7/10
    Working: 3 + 7 = 10 parts, and dogs are 7 of them, so 7/10 of the pets are dogs.
    173/8 of a rugby team's kicks are successful. Write the ratio of successful to unsuccessful kicks in its simplest form, like 3:4.3:5
    Working: 8 − 3 = 5 parts unsuccessful. successful : unsuccessful = 3:5.
    18Three friends share money in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3. What fraction does the biggest share get?1/2
    Working: 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 parts. The biggest share is 3/6 = 1/2 of the total.
    19In a paint mix the ratio of red to white is 2 : 5. What fraction of the mix is red? Give your answer as a fraction, like 3/10.2/7
    Working: 2 + 5 = 7 parts, and red is 2 of them, so 2/7 of the mix is red.
    20A drawing has the scale 1 : 100. A line on the drawing is 5 cm long. How long is the real thing, in metres?5
    Working: 5 × 100 = 500 cm = 5 m.
    21A map has the scale 1 : 50 000. A footpath is 3 cm long on the map. How long is it in real life, in kilometres?1.5
    Working: 3 × 50 000 = 150 000 cm. 150 000 ÷ 100 = 1500 m, and 1500 ÷ 1000 = 1.5 km.
    22A model railway has the scale 1 : 76. A real locomotive is 15.2 m long. How long is the model, in centimetres?20
    Working: 15.2 m = 1520 cm. 1520 ÷ 76 = 20 cm.
    23A recipe for 6 pancakes uses 300 ml of milk. How many millilitres of milk do you need for 15 pancakes? [Figure: Double number line for pancakes and milk]750
    Working: One pancake needs 300 ÷ 6 = 50 ml. For 15 pancakes: 15 × 50 = 750 ml.
    24On a 1 : 20 plan, a kitchen worktop is 15 cm long. How long is the real worktop, in metres?3
    Working: 15 × 20 = 300 cm = 3 m.
    25A plumber has a 2.4 m length of pipe. She cuts it into pieces that are each 30 cm long. How many pieces can she cut?8
    Working: 2.4 m = 240 cm. 240 ÷ 30 = 8 pieces.
    26The exchange rate is £1 = &euro;1.15. How many euros do you get for £80? [Figure: Double number line for tiles and cost]92
    Working: 80 × 1.15 = 92, so you get &euro;92.
    274 pens cost £3.20. How much do 7 pens cost, in pounds? Type it like 4.50 or 4.5.5.6
    Working: £3.20 ÷ 4 = £0.80 for one pen. 7 × 0.80 = £5.60, so 5.6 in pounds.
    28A train travels 150 miles in 2 hours at a steady speed. How far does it go in 3.5 hours?262.5
    Working: 150 ÷ 2 = 75 miles in one hour. 75 × 3.5 = 262.5 miles.
    29In a game you earn 120 points every 5 minutes, at a steady rate. How many points do you earn in 12 minutes?288
    Working: 120 ÷ 5 = 24 points a minute. 24 × 12 = 288 points.
    30Which of these is in direct proportion?The cost of 3, 6 or 9 tins of paint
    Working: Identical tins of paint cost the same each, so 6 tins cost twice 3 tins. The taxi has a fixed fee and a child does not grow at a steady rate.
    313 m of copper pipe costs £12.60. How much does 5 m cost, in pounds?21
    Working: £12.60 ÷ 3 = £4.20 for 1 m. 5 × 4.20 = £21.
    3240 litres of diesel cost £62. How much do 25 litres cost, in pounds? Type it like 12.50.38.75
    Working: £62 ÷ 40 = £1.55 a litre. 25 × 1.55 = £38.75.
    334 pencils cost 88p. How many pence does one pencil cost?22
    Working: 88 ÷ 4 = 22p.
    34Which pack is the best value?Pack B: 400 g for £1.48
    Working: Per 100 g: A is 100p ÷ 2.5 = 40p. B is 148p ÷ 4 = 37p. C is 420p ÷ 10 = 42p. Pack B is cheapest per 100 g.
    35A 2.5 litre tin of paint costs £18. A 5 litre tin costs £32.50. How much cheaper per litre, in pounds, is the larger tin? Type it like 0.55.0.7
    Working: 2.5 litres: £18 ÷ 2.5 = £7.20 per litre. 5 litres: £32.50 ÷ 5 = £6.50 per litre. The difference is 7.20 − 6.50 = £0.7 (70p).
    36A multipack of 6 cans costs £2.70. A pack of 10 cans costs £4.80. What is the price per can, in pence, of the cheaper deal? [Figure: Forwards: share 60 in the ratio 2 to 3]45
    Working: 6 cans: 270p ÷ 6 = 45p each. 10 cans: 480p ÷ 10 = 48p each. The cheaper deal is 45p a can.
    37Two friends share money in the ratio 2 : 3. The smaller share is £14. What was the total, in pounds? [Figure: Bar model with a known smaller share]35
    Working: 2 parts are £14, so one part is £7. The total is 5 parts: 5 × 7 = £35.
    38In a bag of counters the ratio of red to blue is 3 : 4. There are 15 red counters. How many blue counters are there?20
    Working: 3 parts are 15, so one part is 5. Blue is 4 × 5 = 20 counters.
    39Mortar is made with sand and cement in the ratio 4 : 1. A builder uses 28 buckets of sand. How many buckets of mortar mix is that altogether?35
    Working: 4 parts of sand are 28 buckets, so one part is 7 buckets. Cement is 7 buckets. Total: 28 + 7 = 35 buckets.
    40Two shares are in the ratio 3 : 7. The difference between the shares is £48. How much is the larger share, in pounds?84
    Working: The difference is 7 − 3 = 4 parts, so 4 parts are £48 and one part is £12. The larger share is 7 × 12 = £84. (The smaller is £36.)
    41Two sisters share money in the ratio 5 : 3. The older sister gets £18 more than the younger. How much money did they share in total, in pounds?72
    Working: The difference is 5 − 3 = 2 parts = £18, so one part is £9. Total: 8 parts × 9 = £72. (Older £45, younger £27.)
    42Two shares are in the ratio 2 : 5. The larger share is 35. What is the total?49
    Working: 5 parts are 35, so one part is 7. The total is 2 + 5 = 7 parts: 7 × 7 = 49.
    43Zoe works 24 shift hours and Ben works 40 in a week. Write the ratio of Zoe's hours to Ben's in its simplest form, like 2:3.3:5
    Working: The HCF of 24 and 40 is 8. 24 ÷ 8 = 3 and 40 ÷ 8 = 5, so the ratio is 3:5.
    44Dev says: To share £35 in the ratio 2 : 5, I do 35 ÷ 2 = 17.5 and 35 ÷ 5 = 7. What went wrong?He divided by each number in the ratio. He should divide by 2 + 5 = 7.
    Working: 2 + 5 = 7 parts. One part is 35 ÷ 7 = 5, so the shares are 2 × 5 = £10 and 5 × 5 = £25. Check: 10 + 25 = 35 ✓.
    45Ana says: 3 : 5 is the same as 6 : 8, because I added 3 to both sides. What went wrong?She added 3 to both sides. She should multiply both sides by the same number.
    Working: 3 × 2 = 6, so 5 × 2 = 10. The ratio 3 : 5 is the same as 6 : 10, not 6 : 8.
    46Lee says: The ratio 1 : 4 means 1/4 of the total is the first share. What went wrong?The total is 5 parts, so the first share is 1/5.
    Working: 1 + 4 = 5 parts, so the first share is 1/5 of the total. 1/4 would be the ratio 1 : 3.
    47Simplify 45 : 60 fully, like 2:3.3:4
    Working: The HCF of 45 and 60 is 15. 45 ÷ 15 = 3 and 60 ÷ 15 = 4, so 3:4.
    48Share 90 in the ratio 4 : 5. What is the larger share?50
    Working: 4 + 5 = 9 parts, so one part is 10. The larger share is 5 × 10 = 50.
    493/8 of a class are girls. Write the ratio of girls to boys in its simplest form, like 2:3.3:5
    Working: Girls are 3 parts out of 8, so boys are 5 parts. girls : boys = 3:5.
    50A cyclist rides 24 km in 90 minutes at a steady speed. How far does she ride in 2 hours?32
    Working: 2 hours = 120 minutes. Every 30 minutes she rides 24 ÷ 3 = 8 km. 120 minutes is 4 lots of 30: 4 × 8 = 32 km.
    51A site plan has the scale 1 : 500. A building is 8.4 cm long on the plan. How long is the real building, in metres?42
    Working: 8.4 × 500 = 4200 cm = 42 m.
    52Which is the better buy: 4 tomatoes for £1.60 or 6 tomatoes for £2.52?4 for £1.60
    Working: 4 tomatoes: £1.60 ÷ 4 = 40p each. 6 tomatoes: £2.52 ÷ 6 = 42p each. The 4 for £1.60 deal is cheaper per tomato.
    53Two shares are in the ratio 5 : 2. The larger share is 45. What is the total?63
    Working: 5 parts are 45, so one part is 9. Total: 7 parts × 9 = 63.
    54Write the ratio 750 ml : 1.5 litres in its simplest form, like 1:3.1:2
    Working: 1.5 litres = 1500 ml. 750 : 1500, divide both sides by 750, gives 1:2.
    55Sofia mixes white paint and blue paint in the ratio 5 : 2. She needs 14 litres of mixed paint. How many litres of blue paint does she use? [Figure: Bar model for the paint mix]4
    1. M1 for 5 + 2 = 7 parts
    2. M1 for 14 ÷ 7 = 2 litres for one part
    3. A1 for 2 × 2 = 4 litres of blue
    Working: 5 + 2 = 7 parts. 14 ÷ 7 = 2 litres for one part. Blue is 2 × 2 = 4 litres. (White is 10 litres, and 10 + 4 = 14 ✓.)
    		3
    56A recipe for 8 portions uses 600 g of flour. Mia wants to make 20 portions and has 1.2 kg of flour. How many more grams of flour does she need?300
    1. M1 for 600 ÷ 8 = 75 g for one portion (or the scale factor 20 ÷ 8 = 2.5)
    2. M1 for 75 × 20 = 1500 g
    3. M1 for changing 1.2 kg to 1200 g
    4. A1 for 1500 − 1200 = 300 g
    Working: One portion needs 600 ÷ 8 = 75 g (or the scale factor 20 ÷ 8 = 2.5). 20 portions need 75 × 20 = 1500 g. She has 1.2 kg = 1200 g. So she needs 1500 − 1200 = 300 g more.
    		4
    57There are 28 pupils in a class and 12 of them are boys. Show that the ratio of boys to girls is 3 : 4. Type the ratio in its simplest form, like 2:3.3:4
    1. M1 for girls = 28 − 12 = 16
    2. M1 for boys : girls = 12 : 16
    3. C1 for dividing both sides by 4 to get 3:4
    Working: Girls = 28 − 12 = 16. So boys : girls = 12 : 16. Divide both sides by 4 to get 3:4. So the ratio is 3 : 4, as the question says.
    		3
    58a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 4 : 5. Write a : b : c using whole numbers, like 2:3:4.8:12:15
    Working: Make b equal 12. a : b = 2 : 3 = 8 : 12. b : c = 4 : 5 = 12 : 15. So a : b : c = 8:12:15. Check: 8 : 12 = 2 : 3 ✓ and 12 : 15 = 4 : 5 ✓.
    59A bottle holds 10 litres of squash mix (a fruit drink you add water to), made from concentrate and water in the ratio 1 : 4. How many litres of water must be added to change the ratio to 1 : 9?10
    Working: 1 + 4 = 5 parts, so the 10 litres are 2 litres concentrate and 8 litres water. The concentrate stays 2 litres. For 1 : 9 the water must be 9 × 2 = 18 litres. Add 18 − 8 = 10 litres of water.
    60Ali and Bea's ages are in the ratio 2 : 3. In 10 years the ratio will be 3 : 4. How old is Ali now?20
    Working: 4(2x + 10) = 3(3x + 10), so 8x + 40 = 9x + 30, which gives x = 10. Ali is 2 × 10 = 20 now, and Bea is 30. Check: in 10 years they are 30 and 40, and 30 : 40 = 3 : 4 ✓.
    61A map has the scale 1 : 1000. A field has an area of 6 cm² on the map. What is the real area, in m²? (1 m² = 10 000 cm².)600
    Working: Areas are multiplied by 1000 × 1000 = 1 000 000. 6 × 1 000 000 = 6 000 000 cm². 6 000 000 ÷ 10 000 = 600 m².
    62Two people share a total T in the ratio a : b. Which expression gives the first share?T × a/(a + b)
    Working: There are a + b parts. One part is T/(a + b), so the first share is a × T/(a + b) = T × a/(a + b). Try it with T = 60, a = 2, b = 3: 60 × 2/5 = 24 ✓.
    63Antifreeze and water are mixed for a heating system in the ratio 1 : 4. A fitter makes 12 litres of the mix. How many millilitres of antifreeze does she need?2400
    Working: 1 + 4 = 5 parts. 12 litres = 12000 ml, so one part is 12000 ÷ 5 = 2400 ml. Antifreeze is 1 × 2400 = 2400 ml. (Water is 9600 ml, and 2400 + 9600 = 12000 ✓.)
    64A pipework plan is drawn to a scale of 1 : 50. A pipe run measures 18.4 cm on the plan. How long is the real pipe run, in metres?9.2
    Working: 18.4 × 50 = 920 cm. 920 ÷ 100 = 9.2 m. (Sense check: 1 cm on the plan is 50 cm = 0.5 m, so 18.4 cm is about 9 m ✓.)
    65A 4.5 m length of pipe is cut into two pieces in the ratio 2 : 3. How long is the longer piece, in centimetres? [Figure: Bar model for cutting a pipe]270
    Working: 2 + 3 = 5 parts. 4.5 m = 450 cm, so one part is 450 ÷ 5 = 90 cm. The longer piece is 3 × 90 = 270 cm. (The shorter is 180 cm, and 180 + 270 = 450 ✓.)
    66Solder wire is sold on a 500 g reel for £21.00 or a 250 g reel for £11.40. How much cheaper per 100 g, in pence, is the bigger reel? Type it like 12.36
    1. M1 for 2100 ÷ 5 = 420p per 100 g
    2. M1 for 1140 ÷ 2.5 = 456p per 100 g
    3. A1 for 456 − 420 = 36
    Working: Big reel: £21.00 = 2100p, and 2100 ÷ 5 = 420p per 100 g. Small reel: £11.40 = 1140p, and 1140 ÷ 2.5 = 456p per 100 g. Difference: 456 − 420 = 36p per 100 g.
    		3
    67A heating system holds 140 litres of water. The label says to add 250 ml of inhibitor for every 50 litres of water. How many litres of inhibitor does the system need? Type it like 0.35.0.7
    Working: 140 ÷ 50 = 2.8 lots. 2.8 × 250 = 700 ml. 700 ml ÷ 1000 = 0.7 litres.
    68An electrician shares a 50 m reel of cable between three jobs in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. How many metres go to the biggest job?25
    Working: 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 parts. One part is 50 ÷ 10 = 5 m. The biggest job gets 5 × 5 = 25 m. (The others get 10 m and 15 m, and 10 + 15 + 25 = 50 ✓.)
    69Twin and earth cable (a common electrical cable) is sold as a 50 m reel for £84.50 or a 100 m reel for £152.00. How much cheaper per metre, in pence, is the bigger reel? Type it like 12.17
    1. M1 for 8450 ÷ 50 = 169p per metre
    2. M1 for 15200 ÷ 100 = 152p per metre
    3. A1 for 169 − 152 = 17
    Working: Small reel: £84.50 = 8450p, and 8450 ÷ 50 = 169p per metre. Big reel: £152.00 = 15200p, and 15200 ÷ 100 = 152p per metre. Difference: 169 − 152 = 17p per metre.
    		3
    70A stylist mixes hair colour and developer in the ratio 1 : 2. She makes 90 ml of the mix altogether. How many millilitres of developer does she use? [Figure: Bar model for the colour mix]60
    Working: 1 + 2 = 3 parts. 90 ÷ 3 = 30 ml for one part. Developer is 2 × 30 = 60 ml. (Colour is 30 ml, and 30 + 60 = 90 ✓.)
    71The tip pool (all the tips the customers leave) one Saturday is £48. It is shared between two stylists in the ratio 3 : 2, with the larger share to the stylist who worked longer. How many pounds does the larger share come to? Type it like 12.50.28.80 (also accept: 28.8)
    Working: 3 + 2 = 5 parts. 48 ÷ 5 = £9.60 for one part. The larger share is 3 × 9.60 = £28.80. (The smaller share is £19.20, and 28.80 + 19.20 = £48.00 ✓.)
    72A salon mixes colour, developer and conditioner in the ratio 2 : 3 : 1. It needs 240 ml of the mix altogether for a long-hair client. How many millilitres of developer does it need?120
    1. M1 for 2 + 3 + 1 = 6 parts
    2. M1 for 240 ÷ 6 = 40 ml for one part
    3. A1 for 3 × 40 = 120 ml of developer
    Working: 2 + 3 + 1 = 6 parts. 240 ÷ 6 = 40 ml for one part. Developer is 3 × 40 = 120 ml. (Colour 80 ml and conditioner 40 ml: 80 + 120 + 40 = 240 ✓.)
    		3
    73Shampoo comes in two sizes: 400 ml for £4.80 or 1 litre for £10.50. Which is the better buy?The 1 litre bottle
    Working: £4.80 = 480p, and 480 ÷ 400 = 1.2p per ml. £10.50 = 1050p, and 1050 ÷ 1000 = 1.05p per ml. The 1 litre bottle is the better buy.
    74The colour to developer ratio must be 2 : 3. A trainee has 84 ml of colour and 150 ml of developer. What is the largest amount of mix, in millilitres, she can make using the right ratio?210
    1. M1 for 84 ÷ 2 = 42 ml for one part
    2. M1 for 3 × 42 = 126 ml, and checking 126 is less than 150
    3. A1 for 84 + 126 = 210 ml
    Working: Colour is the 2 in the ratio: 84 ÷ 2 = 42 ml for one part. Developer needed: 3 × 42 = 126 ml, and she has 150 ml, so there is enough. The mix is 84 + 126 = 210 ml. (If she used all the developer she would need 100 ml of colour, but she has only 84 ml.)
    		3
