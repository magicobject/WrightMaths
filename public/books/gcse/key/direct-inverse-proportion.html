Direct and inverse proportion: answers

Answers for Direct and inverse proportion
No.QuestionAnswerMarks
16 identical bags of sand cost £30. How much does 1 bag cost? Give your answer in pounds, like 5.00.5.00 (also accept: 5)
Working: 30 ÷ 6 = 5, so 1 bag costs £5.00.
2Write the ratio 12 : 18 in its simplest form. Type it like 2:3.2:3
Working: The highest common factor of 12 and 18 is 6. 12 ÷ 6 = 2 and 18 ÷ 6 = 3, so the ratio is 2 : 3.
3A straight line goes through (0, 0) and (2, 6). What is its gradient?3
Working: Rise = 6, run = 2. Gradient = 6 ÷ 2 = 3.
4A line has the equation y = 2x. What is y when x = 5?10
Working: y = 2 × 5 = 10.
5y is directly proportional to x. When x = 4, y = 20. Work out k.5
Working: k = y ÷ x = 20 ÷ 4 = 5.
6y = 6x. Work out y when x = 7.42
Working: y = 6 × 7 = 42.
7A merchant sells building sand in direct proportion to its weight. 3 bags cost £21. How much do 5 bags cost? Give your answer in pounds, like 35.00.35.00 (also accept: 35)
Working: 1 bag costs 21 ÷ 3 = £7. 5 bags cost 7 × 5 = 35, so £35.00.
8y is directly proportional to x. Find the missing value in the table.24
Working: k = 6 ÷ 2 = 3 (and 15 ÷ 5 = 3). The missing y = 3 × 8 = 24.
9A van uses 12 litres of diesel to drive 150 miles. Assume the amount of diesel is in direct proportion to the distance. How many litres does it use for 400 miles?32
Working: For 1 mile: 12 ÷ 150 = 0.08 litres. For 400 miles: 0.08 × 400 = 32 litres.
102.5 litres of paint cover 30 m² of wall. What area do 4 litres cover? Answer in m².48
Working: 1 litre covers 30 ÷ 2.5 = 12 m². 4 litres cover 12 × 4 = 48 m².
11y is directly proportional to x. What happens to y when x is doubled? [Figure: Cost of cable against length]y doubles
Working: In y = kx, replacing x with 2x gives k × 2x = 2 × kx. So y doubles too.
12The straight line goes through the origin. It shows the cost y (£) of x metres of cable. Work out k, the cost of 1 metre. [Figure: A cost graph with one point marked]5
Working: k = 15 ÷ 3 = 5, so 1 metre costs £5.
13Which statement must be true for a graph of direct proportion?It is a straight line through (0, 0)
Working: Direct proportion means y = kx, which gives a straight line through the origin.
14The straight line through the origin shows the pay y (£) for x hours of work. How much is the pay for 8 hours? Give your answer in pounds. [Figure: A pay graph]96
Working: k = 60 ÷ 5 = 12, so the pay is £12 per hour. For 8 hours: 12 × 8 = 96, so £96.
15Which line shows direct proportion? [Figure: Two straight lines labelled A and B]Line A
Working: Line A starts at the origin, so y = kx and it is direct proportion. Line B does not.
16A straight line through the origin also goes through the point (6, 15). Write the rule for y in terms of x, like 4x.2.5x
Working: k = 15 ÷ 6 = 2.5. The rule is y = 2.5x.
17y is directly proportional to x, so the graph is a straight line through the origin. When x = 8, y = 28. Find y when x = 10. [Figure: Workers and days]35
Working: k = 28 ÷ 8 = 3.5. When x = 10, y = 3.5 × 10 = 35.
18y is inversely proportional to x. When x = 4, y = 6. Work out k.24
Working: k = x × y = 4 × 6 = 24.
19y = 36 ÷ x. Work out y when x = 9.4
Working: y = 36 ÷ 9 = 4.
204 painters take 9 days to paint a block of flats. How many days would 6 painters take, working at the same rate?6
Working: Total work = 4 × 9 = 36 painter-days. For 6 painters: 36 ÷ 6 = 6 days.
21y is inversely proportional to x. Find the missing value in the table.6
Working: k = 2 × 36 = 72 (and 4 × 18 = 72). The missing y = 72 ÷ 12 = 6.
22A pipe delivering 12 litres per minute fills a tank in 30 minutes. A bigger pipe delivers 20 litres per minute. How many minutes does it take to fill the same tank?18
Working: The tank holds 12 × 30 = 360 litres. At 20 litres per minute: 360 ÷ 20 = 18 minutes.
23Which pair is in inverse proportion? [Figure: Three kinds of graph]Tilers and days to tile one floor
Working: More tilers share the same job, so each day more gets done and the job takes fewer days. Tilers and days are in inverse proportion.
24Which describes this table?Direct proportion
Working: y ÷ x = 6, 6 and 6, so y = 6x. It is direct proportion.
25Which describes this table?Inverse proportion
Working: x × y = 24, 24 and 24, so it is inverse proportion with k = 24.
26Which describes this table?Neither
Working: y ÷ x gives 3 and 2.5. x × y gives 3 and 10. Neither is fixed, so it is neither direct nor inverse proportion.
27One of these graphs shows inverse proportion. Type its letter, A, B or C. [Figure: Three graphs labelled A, B and C]B
Working: Graph B is the smooth falling curve, so it shows inverse proportion.
28x and y are in inverse proportion. The table shows some values. Work out k, where k = x × y.200
Working: k = 5 × 40 = 200. Check: 10 × 20 = 200 and 20 × 10 = 200.
29Which of these is NOT in direct proportion?A taxi fare of £4 plus £2 each mile
Working: The taxi fare is £4 even for 0 miles, so doubling the miles does not double the fare. Its graph is a straight line that misses the origin.
30A pancake recipe for 6 people uses 240 g of flour. How much flour is needed for 9 people? Answer in grams.360
Working: Scale factor = 9 ÷ 6 = 1.5. Flour = 240 × 1.5 = 360 g.
31A recipe for 4 people uses 2 eggs. How many eggs does it need for 10 people?5
Working: 1 person needs 2 ÷ 4 = 0.5 eggs. 10 people need 0.5 × 10 = 5 eggs.
32A concrete mix needs 7 bags of cement for every 1 m³. A job needs 2.5 m³. How many whole bags must be bought?18
Working: 7 × 2.5 = 17.5 bags. You cannot buy half a bag, and 17 would not be enough, so buy 18 bags.
33A recipe is doubled. Which amount does NOT simply double?The time in the oven
Working: Ingredients are in direct proportion to the number of servings. The oven time depends on the shape and thickness, so it does not simply double.
341 litre of paint covers 12 m². The walls of a room have an area of 78 m². How many litres are needed? Give your answer in litres.6.5
Working: 78 ÷ 12 = 6.5 litres.
35A recipe for 8 people uses 600 ml of stock. You only have 450 ml of stock. How many people can you cook for?6
Working: 1 person needs 600 ÷ 8 = 75 ml. 450 ÷ 75 = 6, so you can cook for 6 people.
36y = 3x². Work out y when x = 4.48
Working: 4² = 16, then 3 × 16 = 48.
37y is proportional to x². When x = 5, y = 50. Work out k.2
Working: 50 = k × 5² = 25k, so k = 50 ÷ 25 = 2.
38y is proportional to x². When x = 2, y = 12. Find y when x = 6.108
Working: k = 12 ÷ 4 = 3. y = 3 × 6² = 3 × 36 = 108.
39y = 2x² and x is positive. Work out x when y = 72.6
Working: 72 = 2x², so x² = 36 and x = 6.
40The cost of a square tile is proportional to the square of its side length. A tile with a side of 10 cm costs £2. How much does a tile with a side of 30 cm cost? Give your answer in pounds, like 18.00.18.00 (also accept: 18)
Working: k = 2 ÷ 10² = 0.02. Cost = 0.02 × 30² = 0.02 × 900 = 18, so £18.00.
41y is proportional to x². If x is doubled, what happens to y?It is multiplied by 4
Working: (2x)² = 4x², so y is multiplied by 4.
42y is inversely proportional to x². When x = 3, y = 4. Find y when x = 6.1
Working: k = y × x² = 4 × 9 = 36. When x = 6: y = 36 ÷ 36 = 1.
43y is directly proportional to x. When x = 7, y = 21. Write the rule for y, like 4x.3x
Working: k = 21 ÷ 7 = 3. The rule is y = 3x.
44y is inversely proportional to x. When x = 8, y = 5. Write the rule for y, like 12/x.40/x
Working: k = x × y = 8 × 5 = 40. The rule is y = 40 ÷ x.
45y = 4x. Work out x when y = 36.9
Working: 36 = 4x, so x = 36 ÷ 4 = 9.
46y = 60 ÷ x. Work out x when y = 12.5
Working: 12 = 60 ÷ x, so x = 60 ÷ 12 = 5.
47A 6 m board costs £15. The price is in direct proportion to the length. What length of board costs £22.50? Answer in metres.9
Working: 1 m costs 15 ÷ 6 = £2.50. Length = 22.50 ÷ 2.50 = 9 m.
484 workers take 9 days to resurface a car park. How many workers are needed to finish it in 3 days? Assume everyone works at the same rate.12
Working: Total work = 4 × 9 = 36 worker-days. Workers needed = 36 ÷ 3 = 12.
49Dan says: 4 workers take 6 days, so 8 workers take 12 days. What went wrong?He doubled the days, but they should be halved
Working: 4 × 6 = 24 worker-days. With 8 workers: 24 ÷ 8 = 3 days. Dan used direct proportion, but this is inverse proportion.
50Eve says: y is directly proportional to x, and y = 10 when x = 4. So k = 4 ÷ 10 = 0.4. What went wrong?She divided x by y, not y by x
Working: k = y ÷ x = 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5. Eve divided the wrong way round.
51Ida says: a taxi charges £3 to start, then £2 for each mile. 5 miles cost £13, so 10 miles cost double, £26. What went wrong?She treated it as direct proportion, but the £3 is a fixed start charge
Working: 10 miles cost 3 + 2 × 10 = £23. The fare is not directly proportional to the miles, because its graph does not pass through the origin.
528 bags of cement cost £68. Which method finds the cost of 12 bags?Find 1 bag first, then times 12
Working: This is direct proportion. 1 bag costs 68 ÷ 8 = £8.50, and 12 bags cost 8.50 × 12.
538 bags of cement cost £68. How much do 12 bags cost? Give your answer in pounds, like 102.00.102.00 (also accept: 102)
Working: 1 bag costs 68 ÷ 8 = 8.50. 12 bags cost 8.50 × 12 = 102, so £102.00.
543 bricklayers build a garden wall in 10 days. How many days would 5 bricklayers take, working at the same rate?6
Working: Total = 3 × 10 = 30 bricklayer-days. 30 ÷ 5 = 6 days.
55y = 2.5x. Work out y when x = 12.30
Working: y = 2.5 × 12 = 30.
56x and y are in inverse proportion, and x × y = 60. Work out y when x = 15.4
Working: y = 60 ÷ x = 60 ÷ 15 = 4.
57A recipe for 8 people uses 600 g of potatoes. How many grams are needed for 5 people?375
Working: 600 ÷ 8 = 75 g for 1 person. 75 × 5 = 375 g.
58Two identical pumps empty a flooded cellar in 90 minutes. How long would three of the same pumps take? Answer in minutes.60
Working: Total = 2 × 90 = 180 pump-minutes. 180 ÷ 3 = 60 minutes.
59The voltage drop along a cable is directly proportional to its length. Over 25 m of cable the drop is 1.8 V. What is the drop over 40 m of the same cable? Answer in volts.2.88
Working: Drop per metre = 1.8 ÷ 25 = 0.072 V. Over 40 m: 0.072 × 40 = 2.88 V.
60A 2 kW heater costs 56p an hour to run. At the same price per unit of electricity, how much does a 3 kW heater cost to run for an hour? Answer in pence.84
Working: Cost per kW = 56 ÷ 2 = 28p. For 3 kW: 28 × 3 = 84p.
616 bags of cement make 0.9 m³ of concrete. How many bags are needed for 2.1 m³?14
Working: Bags for 1 m³ = 6 ÷ 0.9 = 6.67…. For 2.1 m³: 6 ÷ 0.9 × 2.1 = 14 bags. Check: 2.1 ÷ 0.9 = 2.33… lots of 6, and 6 × 2.33… = 14.
624 plasterers take 9 days to plaster a house. The deadline is 6 days. How many plasterers are needed, working at the same rate?6
Working: Total = 4 × 9 = 36 plasterer-days. 36 ÷ 6 = 6 plasterers.
63Decking boards are in direct proportion to the area covered. 9 boards cover 3.24 m². How many boards are needed to cover 10.8 m²?30
Working: One board covers 3.24 ÷ 9 = 0.36 m². Boards = 10.8 ÷ 0.36 = 30.
64A 1.5 kW heater costs 42p to run for one hour. A 2.5 kW heater is used for 4 hours at the same price per unit. How much does it cost in total? Give your answer in pounds, like 3.10.2.80 (also accept: 2.8)
  1. M1 for 42 ÷ 1.5 = 28p per kW per hour
  2. M1 for 28 × 2.5 × 4 = 280p
  3. A1 for £2.80
Working: Cost per kW per hour = 42 ÷ 1.5 = 28p. For 2.5 kW and 4 hours: 28 × 2.5 × 4 = 280p, which is £2.80.

674 tilers take 15 hours to tile a school corridor. Show that 10 tilers, working at the same rate, would take 6 hours.Written answer: see the mark scheme
  1. M1 for 4 × 15 = 60 (tiler-hours)
  2. M1 for 60 ÷ 10
  3. A1 for 6 hours
  4. C1 for stating that this is inverse proportion (more tilers, less time), so 6 is sensible and less than 15
Working: The work is fixed, so tilers × hours is fixed (inverse proportion). 4 × 15 = 60 tiler-hours. With 10 tilers: 60 ÷ 10 = 6 hours, as required. More tilers means less time, so the answer is sensible: 6 is less than 15.

  1. M1 for 7.20 ÷ 4.5 (= 1.60 per kg)
  2. M1 for 1.60 × 7
  3. A1 for £11.20

69x and y are in inverse proportion. The table shows some values. (a) Show that k = 60, where x × y = k. (b) Find the value of y when x = 5. Type your answer to part (b).12
  1. M1 for 4 × 15 = 60 and 10 × 6 = 60
  2. C1 for stating that x × y is always 60, so k = 60
  3. M1 for y = 60 ÷ x
  4. A1 for 12
Working: (a) 4 × 15 = 60, 10 × 6 = 60 and 20 × 3 = 60. Every pair multiplies to 60, so k = 60. (b) y = 60 ÷ x = 60 ÷ 5 = 12.

70Higher y is directly proportional to the square of x. When x = 4, y = 48. Find the value of y when x = 7.147
  1. M1 for y = kx²
  2. M1 for k = 48 ÷ 16 (= 3)
  3. M1 for 3 × 7²
  4. A1 for 147
Working: y = kx². 48 = k × 4² = 16k, so k = 3. When x = 7: y = 3 × 7² = 3 × 49 = 147.

715 machines make 5 widgets in 5 minutes. Every machine works at the same steady rate. How many minutes would 100 machines take to make 100 widgets?5
Working: 5 machines make 5 widgets in 5 minutes, so each machine makes 1 widget in 5 minutes. 100 machines each make 1 widget at the same time, so 100 widgets take 5 minutes.
72Two gears are meshed together. The first gear has 20 teeth and turns 30 times a minute. The second gear has 12 teeth. How many times a minute does the second gear turn?50
Working: Teeth per minute = 20 × 30 = 600. The second gear has 12 teeth, so it turns 600 ÷ 12 = 50 times a minute. Gears are in inverse proportion: fewer teeth means more turns.
73y is directly proportional to x. Show that if x is increased by 20%, then y is also increased by 20%.Written answer: see the mark scheme
Working: y = kx. After a 20% increase, the new x is 1.2x. The new y = k × 1.2x = 1.2 × kx = 1.2y. So the new y is 1.2 times the old y, which is an increase of 20%. This works for any k and any percentage.
