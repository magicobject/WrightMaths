Working: The correct answer is 3,545. Check: 3,545 + 2,458 = 6,003.
33Tom says: “3.5 × 0.2 = 7.” What went wrong?The decimal point was lost from the answer.
Working: 35 × 2 = 70. 3.5 and 0.2 have one digit after the point each, so the answer has two: 0.70 = 0.7. A fifth of 3.5 is 0.7.

42Use the digits 2, 3 and 4 once each to make a two-digit number multiplied by a one-digit number. What is the largest product you can make?128
Working: 43 × 2 = 86, 42 × 3 = 126, 32 × 4 = 128, 34 × 2 = 68, 24 × 3 = 72, 23 × 4 = 92. The largest is 32 × 4 = 128. Putting the biggest digit (4) on its own as the multiplier wins.
