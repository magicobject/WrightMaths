    Answers for Order of operations
    No.QuestionAnswerMarks
    1A gamer starts with 5 coins and wins 2 coins on each of 6 levels. The total is 5 + 2 × 6. How many coins is that?17
    Working: Multiplication comes before addition: 2 × 6 = 12 coins won, then 5 + 12 = 17.
    2Work out 6 × 7.42
    Working: 6 × 7 = 42.
    3Work out 56 − 19.37
    Working: 56 − 19 = 37.
    4Work out 90 ÷ 6. [Figure: The BIDMAS staircase]15
    Working: 90 ÷ 6 = 15.
    5Work out 7 + 2 × 4.15
    Working: 2 × 4 = 8, then 7 + 8 = 15.
    6Work out 20 − 6 ÷ 2.17
    Working: 6 ÷ 2 = 3, then 20 − 3 = 17.
    7A player has 10 lives, loses 2 and then wins 3 back. Work out 10 − 2 + 3.11
    Working: 10 − 2 = 8, then 8 + 3 = 11.
    8Work out 8 ÷ 2 × 4.16
    Working: 8 ÷ 2 = 4, then 4 × 4 = 16.
    9A playlist has 5 songs. You add 6 albums of 3 songs each, then delete 4 songs. Work out 5 + 6 × 3 − 4.19
    Working: 6 × 3 = 18, then 5 + 18 = 23, then 23 − 4 = 19.
    10Zoe mixes 2 batches of paint. Each batch has 5 ml of red and 3 ml of blue. Work out 2 × (5 + 3), the total ml of paint.16
    Working: 5 + 3 = 8, then 2 × 8 = 16.
    11Work out (12 − 4) ÷ 2.4
    Working: 12 − 4 = 8, then 8 ÷ 2 = 4.
    12Work out 3 + 2².7
    Working: 2² = 4, then 3 + 4 = 7.
    13Work out (3 + 2)².25
    Working: 5² = 25.
    14Work out 2 × 3².18
    Working: 3² = 9, then 2 × 9 = 18.
    15Work out (12 + 8)/4. [Figure: Forwards: 2 + 3 × 4 and unknown]5
    Working: 12 + 8 = 20, then 20 ÷ 4 = 5.
    16Where must the brackets go to make 2 + 3 × 4 = 20?(2 + 3) × 4
    Working: (2 + 3) × 4 = 5 × 4 = 20.
    17Which operation makes 6 ? 2 + 3 = 6 true?÷
    Working: 6 ÷ 2 + 3 = 3 + 3 = 6.
    183 × (? + 2) = 21. Find the missing number.5
    Working: 21 ÷ 3 = 7, and 7 − 2 = 5. Check: 3 × (5 + 2) = 21.
    19? ÷ 4 + 1 = 6. Find the missing number.20
    Working: 6 − 1 = 5, and 5 × 4 = 20. Check: 20 ÷ 4 + 1 = 6.
    20Leo works out the total for a relay kit as 9 + 18 ÷ 3 and gets 9. What should the answer be?15
    Working: 18 ÷ 3 = 6, then 9 + 6 = 15. Leo added first (27 ÷ 3 = 9).
    21Ali says: “6 + 4 × 2 = 20.” What went wrong?He added first, not multiplied first.
    Working: He added first, not multiplied first. 4 × 2 = 8, then 6 + 8 = 14.
    22Bea says: “12 ÷ 3 × 2 = 2.” What went wrong?She did 3 × 2 first.
    Working: She did 3 × 2 first. ÷ and × are equal, so go left to right: 12 ÷ 3 = 4, then 4 × 2 = 8.
    23Cal says: “3² = 6.” What went wrong?He doubled the 3.
    Working: He doubled the 3. 3² = 3 × 3 = 9: the little 2 says how many 3s to multiply.
    24Work out 7 + 3 × (8 − 2).25
    Working: 8 − 2 = 6, 3 × 6 = 18, 7 + 18 = 25.
    25Work out 50 − 3² × 2.32
    Working: 3² = 9, 9 × 2 = 18, 50 − 18 = 32.
    26Work out 2² + 3².13
    Working: 4 + 9 = 13.
    27Work out (10 − 4)² ÷ 3.12
    Working: 10 − 4 = 6, 6² = 36, 36 ÷ 3 = 12.
    28Work out 36 ÷ (2 + 4) × 3.18
    Working: 2 + 4 = 6, 36 ÷ 6 = 6, 6 × 3 = 18.
    29(a) [1 mark] Work out 3 + 4 × 5².103
    Working: 5² = 25, 4 × 25 = 100, 3 + 100 = 103.
    30(b) [2 marks] Work out (12 − 3)² ÷ 3.27
    1. M1 for the bracket first: 12 − 3 = 9, then 9² = 81
    2. A1 for 81 ÷ 3 = 27
    Working: 9² = 81 and 81 ÷ 3 = 27.

    31(c) [2 marks] Sam works out 6 + 18 ÷ 3 and gets 8. What is the correct answer?12
    1. M1 for dividing first: 18 ÷ 3 = 6
    2. A1 for 6 + 6 = 12
    Working: 18 ÷ 3 = 6, then 6 + 6 = 12.
    		2
    32Work out (2 + 10)/3 + 4 × 2.12
    Working: 12/3 = 4 and 4 × 2 = 8. 4 + 8 = 12.
    33Work out 2 × 3² ÷ 6 + (−1).2
    Working: 3² = 9, 2 × 9 = 18, 18 ÷ 6 = 3, 3 + (−1) = 2.
    34A classic puzzle: use four 4s, once each, with the usual operations and brackets, to make a target number. For example, 4 × 4 + 4 ÷ 4 = 17. Which sign goes in the square brackets to make 4 × 4 [ ] (4 ÷ 4) = 15?−
    Working: (4 ÷ 4) = 1 and 4 × 4 = 16. To get 15, take 1 away: 4 × 4 − (4 ÷ 4) = 16 − 1 = 15, so the sign is −. Can you make 7, or 0, or 8 with four 4s too?
    35Work out −3², using the standard order of operations.-9
    Working: The power comes before the minus sign: 3² = 9, so −3² = −9.
    36Work out −2² + (−2)².0
    Working: −2² = −4 and (−2)² = 4. −4 + 4 = 0.
    37When x = 6, work out the value of (1/2) x (half of x ).3
    Working: (1/2) × 6 = 3. (By contrast 1/(2 x ) would be 1/12, which is why the brackets matter.)
