    Similar shapes: answers

    54 questions, 5 of them marked (17 marks in all). Question numbers match the page. Use your browser's Print for a copy.

    Open the questions Back to the teacher page
    Answers for Similar shapes
    No.QuestionAnswerMarks
    1A recipe uses 2 cups of flour for 4 people. How many cups of flour does it need for 12 people?6
    Working: 12 people is 3 times as many as 4 people (12 / 4 = 3). So the flour is 3 times as much: 2 × 3 = 6 cups.
    2Write the ratio 12 : 18 in its simplest form. Type it like 2:3.2:3
    Working: The highest common factor of 12 and 18 is 6. 12 / 6 = 2 and 18 / 6 = 3. So the ratio is 2:3.
    3Two angles of a triangle are 50° and 60°. Work out the third angle, in degrees.70
    Working: The angles in a triangle add up to 180°. 50 + 60 = 110. 180 − 110 = 70°.
    4Solve x/5 = 12/3. [Figure: Two rectangles]20
    Working: 12/3 = 4, so x/5 = 4. Multiply both sides by 5: x = 4 × 5 = 20.
    5Which pair of rectangles is similar?3 cm by 5 cm and 6 cm by 10 cm
    Working: 3 to 6 is times 2 and 5 to 10 is times 2. Both sides are multiplied by 2, so these two rectangles are similar.
    6Any two squares are…always similar
    Working: All squares have four right angles and four equal sides. Any square can be scaled up or down to match any other, so any two squares are always similar.
    7Are these two rectangles similar? Type yes or no. [Figure: Two rectangles]no
    Working: Long sides: 9 / 6 = 1.5. Short sides: 7 / 4 = 1.75. The numbers are different, so the rectangles are not similar. The answer is no.
    8Triangle P has sides 3 cm, 4 cm and 5 cm. Triangle Q has sides 6 cm, 8 cm and 10 cm. Are they similar? Type yes or no. [Figure: Two triangles]yes
    Working: 6 / 3 = 2, 8 / 4 = 2 and 10 / 5 = 2. Every side is multiplied by 2, so the triangles are similar. The answer is yes.
    9Which word means exactly the same shape and exactly the same size? [Figure: Rectangles with a missing side]Congruent
    Working: Congruent shapes are the same shape and the same size. Similar shapes are the same shape but can be different sizes.
    10A rectangle is 5 cm by 3 cm. It is enlarged by a scale factor of 4. How long is the long side of the new rectangle, in cm?20
    Working: The long side is 5 cm. 5 × 4 = 20 cm.
    11The two rectangles are similar. Find the length x, in cm. [Figure: Similar rectangles with a missing side]10
    Working: Scale factor = 15 / 6 = 2.5. The 4 cm side matches x. x = 4 × 2.5 = 10 cm.
    12Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of 6 cm (small triangle) and 21 cm (big triangle). What is the scale factor from the small triangle to the big one?3.5
    Working: Scale factor = new / old = 21 / 6 = 3.5.
    13A photo is 12 cm wide and 8 cm tall. It is enlarged so that it is 30 cm wide. How tall is the enlargement, in cm?20
    Working: Scale factor = 30 / 12 = 2.5. Height = 8 × 2.5 = 20 cm.
    14A carpenter makes a model of a shelf unit that is 40 cm wide and 25 cm tall. The real shelf unit is similar and 120 cm wide. How tall is the real unit, in cm? [Figure: A triangle with a line parallel to its base]75
    Working: Scale factor = 120 / 40 = 3. Height = 25 × 3 = 75 cm.
    15Triangle A has angles of 40° and 75°. Triangle B has angles of 40° and 65°. Are the two triangles similar? Type yes or no.yes
    Working: Third angle in A: 180 − 40 − 75 = 65°. Third angle in B: 180 − 40 − 65 = 75°. Both triangles have angles 40°, 65° and 75°. So they are similar. The answer is yes.
    16In triangle ABC the line DE is parallel to BC. AD = 3 cm, AB = 9 cm and DE = 4 cm. Find BC, in cm. [Figure: A triangle with a line parallel to its base]12
    Working: Scale factor = AB / AD = 9 / 3 = 3. BC = DE × 3 = 4 × 3 = 12 cm.
    17A model roof truss is a triangle with a base of 30 cm and a height of 12 cm. The real truss is similar, with a base of 4.5 m. How tall is the real truss, in m? [Figure: A model truss and a real truss]1.8
    Working: 4.5 m = 450 cm. Scale factor = 450 / 30 = 15. Height = 12 × 15 = 180 cm = 1.8 m.
    18A stake and a flagpole both stand upright. Why are the triangles made by them and their shadows similar? [Figure: A plan of a room]The sun's rays are parallel, so the angles match
    Working: At the same moment the sun's rays are parallel. So the angle between ray and ground is the same for both, and both objects make a right angle with the ground. Two matching angles are enough: the triangles are similar.
    19A drawing of a garden uses a scale of 1 : 100. A path is 7 cm long on the drawing. How long is the real path, in m?7
    Working: Real length = 7 × 100 = 700 cm. 700 / 100 = 7 m.
    20A map has a scale of 1 : 50 000. Two towns are 4.5 cm apart on the map. How far apart are they in km?2.25
    Working: Real distance = 4.5 × 50 000 = 225 000 cm = 2250 m = 2.25 km.
    21A plan of a kitchen uses a scale of 1 : 20. The wall that needs skirting board is 18 cm long on the plan. Skirting board costs £4.80 per metre. How much does the skirting board for the wall cost? Give your answer in pounds, like 12.50.17.28
    Working: Real length = 18 × 20 = 360 cm = 3.6 m. Cost = 3.6 × 4.80 = £17.28.
    22A scale of 1 : 200 means… [Figure: Forwards: old length and scale factor to new length]1 cm on the drawing is 200 cm in real life
    Working: In 1 : 200, 1 unit on the drawing stands for 200 of the same unit in real life. So 1 cm on the drawing is 200 cm (2 m) in real life.
    23Corresponding sides of two similar shapes are 8 cm (small shape) and 20 cm (big shape). What is the scale factor from the small shape to the big shape?2.5
    Working: Scale factor = new / old = 20 / 8 = 2.5.
    24The same two shapes: 8 cm (small) and 20 cm (big). What is the scale factor from the big shape to the small shape?0.4
    Working: The small shape is the new one. Scale factor = new / old = 8 / 20 = 0.4. (That is 1 divided by 2.5.)
    25A photo 24 cm wide and 16 cm tall is reduced to a similar copy that is 9 cm wide. How tall is the copy, in cm?6
    Working: Scale factor = 9 / 24 = 0.375. Height = 16 × 0.375 = 6 cm.
    26A rectangle was enlarged by a scale factor of 4 to make a rectangle with a long side of 28 cm. How long was the long side of the original rectangle, in cm?7
    Working: Enlargement = original × 4. So original = 28 / 4 = 7 cm.
    27The big triangle and the small triangle are similar. Find x, in cm. [Figure: A big triangle and a small triangle]9
    Working: The 20 cm side matches the 15 cm side. Scale factor (big to small) = 15 / 20 = 0.75. x matches 12 cm: x = 12 × 0.75 = 9 cm.
    28Higher Two similar rectangles have a length scale factor of 4. The small rectangle has an area of 5 cm². What is the area of the large rectangle, in cm²?80
    Working: Area scale factor = 4² = 16. Area = 5 × 16 = 80 cm².
    29Higher A model of a water tank holds 2 litres. The real tank is similar, with every length 10 times as big. How many litres does the real tank hold?2000
    Working: Volume scale factor = 10^3 = 1000. Volume = 2 × 1000 = 2000 litres.
    30Higher Two similar bottles have heights of 12 cm and 18 cm. The smaller bottle holds 250 ml. How much does the larger bottle hold, in ml?843.75
    Working: Length scale factor = 18 / 12 = 1.5. Volume scale factor = 1.5^3 = 3.375. Volume = 250 × 3.375 = 843.75 ml.
    31Higher Two similar shapes have areas of 12 cm² and 108 cm². A side of the small shape is 5 cm. How long is the matching side of the large shape, in cm? [Figure: Two triangles with matching sides]15
    Working: Area scale factor = 108 / 12 = 9. Length scale factor = square root of 9 = 3. Matching side = 5 × 3 = 15 cm.
    32Which of these is not enough to prove two triangles congruent?All three angles are equal
    Working: Three equal angles only make the triangles similar. A big triangle and a small one can have the same angles, so they are not congruent.
    33Two right-angled triangles each have a hypotenuse of 13 cm and another side of 5 cm. Which condition shows they are congruent?RHS: right angle, hypotenuse, side
    Working: Both triangles have a right angle, equal hypotenuses (13 cm) and one equal side (5 cm). That is RHS.
    34Higher Triangle ABC is congruent to triangle PQR, with A matching P, B matching Q and C matching R. Angle B = 55°. What is angle Q, in degrees?55
    Working: The triangles are congruent, so matching angles are equal. B matches Q, so angle Q = angle B = 55°.
    35Ali says: a 2 cm by 5 cm rectangle and a 6 cm by 10 cm rectangle are similar, because 6 is 3 times 2. What went wrong?He checked only one pair of sides
    Working: 6 / 2 = 3, but 10 / 5 = 2. The scale factors are different, so the rectangles are not similar. Ali must check every pair of matching sides.
    36Bea says: the scale factor from a side of 12 cm to a side of 8 cm is 12 / 8 = 1.5. What went wrong?She divided old by new, not new by old
    Working: The new length is 8 cm and the old length is 12 cm. Scale factor = 8 / 12 = 2/3, which is less than 1 because the new shape is smaller.
    37Higher Cal says: two similar tins have heights in the ratio 1 : 3, so the big tin holds 3 times as much. What went wrong?Volume is multiplied by 3 × 3 × 3 = 27
    Working: Each of the three lengths is multiplied by 3, so the volume is multiplied by 3 × 3 × 3 = 27. The big tin holds 27 times as much.
    38A map has a scale of 1 : 50 000. Two lighthouses are 12 km apart in real life. How far apart are they on the map, in cm?24
    Working: 12 km = 12 × 100 000 = 1 200 000 cm. On the map: 1 200 000 / 50 000 = 24 cm.
    39Triangle A has sides of 4 cm, 6 cm and 9 cm. Triangle B is similar to A, and its shortest side is 10 cm. How long is the longest side of B, in cm?22.5
    Working: Scale factor = 10 / 4 = 2.5. Longest side of B = 9 × 2.5 = 22.5 cm.
    40A small right-angled triangle has shorter sides of 3 cm and 4 cm. A similar triangle has a shortest side of 9 cm. Find the length of the hypotenuse of the larger triangle, in cm.15
    Working: Small hypotenuse: the square root of (3² + 4²) = the square root of 25 = 5 cm. Scale factor = 9 / 3 = 3. Large hypotenuse = 5 × 3 = 15 cm.
    41Skylights take up 15% of a roof with an area of 240 m². How many square metres of the roof are skylights?36
    Working: 15% of 240 = 0.15 × 240 = 36 m².
    42Electrical. A wiring plan uses a scale of 1 : 50. A cable run measures 14.2 cm on the plan. Twin and earth cable costs £1.80 per metre. How much does the cable for the run cost? Give your answer in pounds, like 12.50.12.78
    Working: Real length = 14.2 × 50 = 710 cm = 7.1 m. Cost = 7.1 × 1.80 = £12.78.
    43Plumbing. A heating plan uses a scale of 1 : 20. A pipe run measures 36 cm on the plan. 15 mm copper pipe costs £6.40 per metre. How much does the pipe cost? Give your answer in pounds, like 12.50.46.08
    Working: Real length = 36 × 20 = 720 cm = 7.2 m. Cost = 7.2 × 6.40 = £46.08.
    44Carpentry. A model of a roof truss is 20 cm wide at the base and 8 cm high. The real truss is similar and 6 m wide at the base. How high is the real truss, in m?2.4
    Working: 6 m = 600 cm. Scale factor = 600 / 20 = 30. Height = 8 × 30 = 240 cm = 2.4 m.
    45Shadows. A 2 m spirit level standing upright casts a shadow 1.25 m long. At the same time a chimney casts a shadow 7.5 m long. How tall is the chimney, in m?12
    Working: Scale factor = 7.5 / 1.25 = 6. Height of chimney = 2 × 6 = 12 m.
    46Kitchen fitting. A kitchen plan has a scale of 1 : 10. A worktop is 36 cm long on the plan. Worktop costs £95 per metre. What is the cost of the worktop, in pounds?342
    Working: Real length = 36 × 10 = 360 cm = 3.6 m. Cost = 3.6 × 95 = £342.
    47Triangle PQR is similar to triangle ABC. AB = 6 cm, BC = 9 cm and AC = 12 cm. PQ = 8 cm, and PQ matches AB. Work out the perimeter of triangle PQR, in cm. [Figure: Two similar triangles]36
    1. M1 for the scale factor 8 / 6 (or 4/3)
    2. M1 for finding QR = 12 or PR = 16
    3. A1 for the perimeter, 36
    Working: Scale factor = 8 / 6 = 4/3. QR = 9 × 4/3 = 12 cm and PR = 12 × 4/3 = 16 cm. Perimeter = 8 + 12 + 16 = 36 cm.
    		3
    48A model of a bridge is made to a scale of 1 : 40. The model is 55 cm long. A rope along the real bridge costs £3.20 per metre. Work out the cost of a rope as long as the real bridge. Give your answer in pounds, like 12.50.70.40
    1. M1 for 55 × 40 = 2200 (the real length in cm)
    2. M1 for changing 2200 cm to 22 m
    3. A1 for 22 × 3.20 = 70.40
    Working: Real length = 55 × 40 = 2200 cm. 2200 cm = 22 m. Cost = 22 × 3.20 = £70.40.
    		3
    49In triangle ABC, the line DE is parallel to BC. AD = 6 cm, DB = 3 cm and DE = 8 cm. Show that BC = 12 cm. [Figure: A triangle with a line parallel to its base]Written answer: see the mark scheme
    1. C1 for saying the triangles ADE and ABC are similar, because the angle at A is shared and the corresponding angles are equal (DE is parallel to BC)
    2. M1 for AB = 6 + 3 = 9 cm
    3. M1 for the scale factor 9 / 6 = 1.5 (or 9 / 6)
    4. A1 for BC = 8 × 1.5 = 12 cm shown
    Working: DE is parallel to BC, so the angles ADE and ABC are equal (corresponding angles). The angle at A is shared. So triangles ADE and ABC are similar. AB = 6 + 3 = 9 cm. The scale factor from ADE to ABC = 9 / 6 = 1.5. BC = DE × 1.5 = 8 × 1.5 = 12 cm.
    		4
    50Higher [3 marks] Two paint tins are similar. The small tin is 8 cm tall and the large tin is 12 cm tall. The label on the small tin has an area of 60 cm². Work out the area of the label on the large tin, in cm².135
    1. M1 for the length scale factor 12 / 8 = 1.5
    2. M1 for the area scale factor 1.5² = 2.25
    3. A1 for 60 × 2.25 = 135
    Working: Length scale factor = 12 / 8 = 1.5. Area scale factor = 1.5² = 2.25. Area = 60 × 2.25 = 135 cm².
    		3
    51Higher [4 marks] Two water tanks are similar. The small tank is 50 cm tall and holds 80 litres. The large tank holds 270 litres. Work out the height of the large tank, in cm.75
    1. M1 for the volume scale factor 270 / 80 = 3.375
    2. M1 for the cube root of 3.375 = 1.5
    3. M1 for 50 × 1.5
    4. A1 for 75
    Working: Volume scale factor = 270 / 80 = 3.375. Length scale factor = the cube root of 3.375 = 1.5. Height = 50 × 1.5 = 75 cm.
    		4
    52A sheet of A4 paper is 29.7 cm by 21 cm. Fold it in half across its long side to make A5, which is 21 cm by 14.85 cm. For each sheet, work out the long side divided by the short side, to 2 decimal places. What do you notice? (This is why you can enlarge A5 to A4 on a photocopier without any gaps.)1.41
    Working: A4: 29.7 / 21 = 1.414… ≈ 1.41. A5: 21 / 14.85 = 1.414… ≈ 1.41. The ratio is the same, so the two sheets are similar. Each fold makes a smaller sheet of the same shape, and that works because the ratio of long to short is the square root of 2.
    53You stand 2 m from a mirror lying flat on the ground. The mirror is 12 m from the foot of a tree. You can just see the top of the tree in the mirror. Your eyes are 1.6 m above the ground. How tall is the tree, in m? (Light bounces off a flat mirror at the same angle it arrives, so the two triangles are similar.) [Figure: A mirror used to measure a tree]9.6
    Working: The triangles are similar (equal angles at the mirror, and a right angle at the ground at each end). Scale factor = 12 / 2 = 6. Height of tree = 1.6 × 6 = 9.6 m.
    54Higher A cube has sides of 4 cm. A second cube has exactly twice the volume. By what scale factor are the side lengths multiplied? Give your answer to 2 decimal places. (Is it 2? Check what doubling the sides does to the volume.)1.26
    Working: The volume scale factor is 2, so k^3 = 2. k = the cube root of 2 = 1.2599… ≈ 1.26. The new side is 4 × 1.26 = 5.04 cm.
