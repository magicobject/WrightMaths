    Percentages: answers

    69 questions, 8 of them marked (22 marks in all). Question numbers match the page. Use your browser's Print for a copy.

    Answers for Percentages
    No.QuestionAnswerMarks
    1Simplify 25/100. Give your answer as a fraction, like 3/5.1/4
    Working: 25 ÷ 25 = 1 and 100 ÷ 25 = 4, so 25/100 = 1/4.
    2Work out 1/5 of 60.12
    Working: 60 ÷ 5 = 12.
    3Write 0.35 as a fraction in its simplest form, like 3/5.7/20
    Working: 0.35 = 35/100. Divide the numerator and the denominator by 5: 7/20.
    4Fill the gap: 3/5 = (?)/100. What number goes where the ? is? [Figure: A hundred square]60
    Working: 5 × 20 = 100, so 3 × 20 = 60. 3/5 = 60/100.
    5The whole bar is 100%. It is cut into equal parts and some are shaded. What percentage is shaded? Give your answer like 12%. [Figure: A bar in ten equal parts]70% (also accept: 70)
    Working: 100% ÷ 10 parts = 10% each. 7 shaded parts is 7 × 10% = 70%.
    6Write 0.6 as a percentage. Give your answer like 12%.60% (also accept: 60)
    Working: 0.6 × 100 = 60, so 0.6 = 60%.
    7Write 35% as a decimal.0.35
    Working: 35 ÷ 100 = 0.35.
    8Write 35% as a fraction in its simplest form, like 3/5.7/20
    Working: 35% = 35/100. Divide the numerator and the denominator by 5: 7/20.
    9Write 3/8 as a percentage. Give your answer like 12.5%.37.5% (also accept: 37.5)
    Working: 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375 and 0.375 × 100 = 37.5, so 3/8 = 37.5%.
    10In a survey, 9 out of 25 gamers chose a blue controller. What percentage chose blue? Give your answer like 12%.36% (also accept: 36)
    Working: 25 × 4 = 100 and 9 × 4 = 36, so 9/25 = 36/100 = 36%.
    11Which is the biggest: 0.6, 55% or 5/8? [Figure: A bar for 240 pounds in ten parts]5/8
    Working: 0.6 = 60%, 55% stays as it is and 5/8 = 0.625 = 62.5%. The biggest is 5/8.
    12What is 10% of £80?8
    Working: 80 ÷ 10 = £8.
    13What is 25% of 64 kg? Give your answer in kg.16
    Working: 25% = 1/4, and 64 ÷ 4 = 16 kg.
    14The bar stands for £150 and is cut into 10 equal parts. Four parts are shaded. How much money do the shaded parts stand for? Answer in pounds. [Figure: A bar for 150 pounds in ten parts]60
    Working: 150 ÷ 10 = 15 in each part. 4 × 15 = £60, which is 40% of £150.
    15A game has 250 levels. Kai has finished 18% of them. How many levels has Kai finished? You can use a calculator.45
    Working: 18% = 0.18 and 0.18 × 250 = 45 levels.
    16A plumber’s materials cost £480. The copper pipe is 35% of the cost. How much does the pipe cost? Answer in pounds.168
    Working: 10% = 48, so 30% = 144 and 5% = 24. 144 + 24 = £168. Check: 0.35 × 480 = 168.
    17Use a calculator to work out 7.5% of £64. Give your answer in pounds, like 4.50.4.8 (also accept: 4.80)
    Working: 7.5% = 0.075 and 0.075 × 64 = 4.8, so £4.80.
    18Which calculation finds 12% of 350?0.12 × 350
    Working: 12% = 0.12, so 12% of 350 = 0.12 × 350 = 42.
    19A team won 6 of their 8 matches. What percentage did they win? Give your answer like 12.5%.75% (also accept: 75)
    Working: 6/8 = 0.75, and 0.75 × 100 = 75%.
    20A cake mix weighs 500 g. 75 g of it is cocoa. What percentage of the mix is cocoa? Give your answer like 12%.15% (also accept: 15)
    Working: 75/500 = 0.15, and 0.15 × 100 = 15%.
    21Sam earns £150 a week and saves £18. What percentage of the wage (the money Sam earns) does Sam save? Give your answer like 12%.12% (also accept: 12)
    Working: 18 ÷ 150 = 0.12, and 0.12 × 100 = 12%.
    22A cable is 3 m long. A piece 60 cm long is cut off. What percentage of the cable is cut off? Give your answer like 12%.20% (also accept: 20)
    Working: 3 m = 300 cm. 60/300 = 0.2, and 0.2 × 100 = 20%.
    23In one quiz Maya got 33 out of 40. What percentage is that? Give your answer like 12.5%.82.5% (also accept: 82.5)
    Working: 33 ÷ 40 = 0.825, and 0.825 × 100 = 82.5%.
    24Who did best? Ali scored 17 out of 20. Bea scored 33 out of 40. Cat scored 41 out of 50.Ali
    Working: Ali 17 out of 20 = 85%. Bea 33 out of 40 = 82.5%. Cat 41 out of 50 = 82%. Ali scored highest.
    25What single number do you multiply by to increase an amount by 8%? Give it as a decimal, like 1.25.1.08
    Working: 100% + 8% = 108% = 1.08.
    26What single number do you multiply by to decrease an amount by 30%? Give it as a decimal.0.7
    Working: 100% − 30% = 70% = 0.7.
    27Increase £50 by 10%. Answer in pounds.55
    Working: 1.1 × 50 = £55.
    28A bike costs £250 before VAT. VAT is 20%. What is the price with VAT? Answer in pounds.300
    Working: 1.2 × 250 = £300. (The VAT is £50.)
    29A £84 hoodie is 25% off in a sale. What is the sale price? Answer in pounds. [Figure: A bar for 84 pounds in four parts]63
    Working: You pay 75% = 0.75. 0.75 × 84 = £63.
    30A game has 1200 players. The number of players falls by 5%. How many players are there now?1140 (also accept: 1,140)
    Working: 100% − 5% = 95% = 0.95. 0.95 × 1200 = 1140 players.
    31Which single multiplier decreases an amount by 12%?0.88
    Working: 100% − 12% = 88% = 0.88.
    32Mia puts £600 in an account that pays 3% simple interest a year. How much interest does she earn in 4 years? Answer in pounds. [Figure: Forwards: 80 and unknown]72
    Working: 3% of 600 = £18 a year. 4 × 18 = £72.
    33Trainers cost £63 in a 25% sale. What was the price before the sale? Answer in pounds. [Figure: A bar for the original price]84
    Working: 75% = 63, so 25% = 21 and 100% = 84. Or 63 ÷ 0.75 = £84.
    34A boiler costs £540 with 20% VAT included. What is the price without VAT? Answer in pounds.450
    Working: 540 ÷ 1.2 = £450.
    35A rent is now £1248 after a 4% rise. What was the rent before the rise? Answer in pounds.1200
    Working: 1248 ÷ 1.04 = 1200, so the old rent was £1200.
    36A cereal packet says “20% extra free” and now holds 600 g. How many grams did the normal packet hold?500
    Working: 100% + 20% = 120% = 1.2. 600 ÷ 1.2 = 500 g.
    37A game had 800 players last month and 1000 players this month. What is the percentage increase? Give your answer like 12.5%.25% (also accept: 25)
    Working: Change = 1000 − 800 = 200. 200 ÷ 800 = 0.25, and 0.25 × 100 = 25%.
    38An amplifier used to cost £250. It now costs £200. What is the percentage decrease? Give your answer like 12%.20% (also accept: 20)
    Working: Change = 250 − 200 = 50. 50 ÷ 250 = 0.2, and 0.2 × 100 = 20%.
    39A cafe’s daily takings fell from £640 to £560. What is the percentage decrease? Give your answer like 8%.12.5% (also accept: 12.5)
    Working: Fall = 640 − 560 = 80. 80 ÷ 640 = 0.125, and 0.125 × 100 = 12.5%.
    40A shop takes 30% off its prices. You know a sale price. How do you find the original price?Divide the sale price by 0.7
    Working: You pay 70% = 0.7 of the original. To undo that, divide by 0.7.
    41Tom says: a coat costs £48 after 20% off, so the original price was £48 + 20% = £57.60. What went wrong?He found 20% of the sale price, not of the original.
    Working: 20% off £57.60 is 0.8 × 57.60 = £46.08, not £48. The right way is 48 ÷ 0.8 = £60.
    42Priya says: if a price goes up by 10% and then comes down by 10%, you are back where you started. What is wrong with that?The fall is 10% of the new, bigger price, so you end up lower.
    Working: £100 up 10% = £110. Down 10% = 0.9 × 110 = £99. The second 10% is of £110, not £100.
    43Which is smaller: 3/8 or 40%?3/8
    Working: 3/8 = 0.375 = 37.5%, which is less than 40%.
    44Write 0.04 as a percentage. Give your answer like 12%.4% (also accept: 4)
    Working: 0.04 × 100 = 4, so 0.04 = 4%.
    45Without a calculator, work out 17.5% of £80. Answer in pounds.14
    Working: 10% = 8, 5% = 4 and 2.5% = 2. 8 + 4 + 2 = £14.
    46A train fare is £36. It rises by 5%. What is the new fare? Give your answer in pounds, like 4.50.37.8 (also accept: 37.80)
    Working: 1.05 × 36 = 37.8, so £37.80.
    47A TV costs £340 in a 15% sale. What was the price before the sale? Answer in pounds.400
    Working: 100% − 15% = 85% = 0.85. 340 ÷ 0.85 = £400.
    48In a music class, 18 of the 24 students play guitar. What percentage play guitar? Give your answer like 12%.75% (also accept: 75)
    Working: 18/24 = 3/4 = 0.75, and 0.75 × 100 = 75%.
    49A recipe uses 240 g of butter. For a big batch you need 150% of that amount. How many grams of butter is that?360
    Working: 150% = 1.5. 1.5 × 240 = 360 g. (100% is 240 g and 50% is 120 g.)
    50Work out the sale price. Answer in pounds.486
    1. M1 for 450 × 1.2 = 540
    2. M1 for 540 × 0.9
    3. A1 for 486
    Working: 450 × 1.2 = 540 (price with VAT). Then 540 × 0.9 (10% off the price with VAT) = 486. The sale price is £486.
    		3
    51(a) [3 marks] Work out the original price of the bike. Answer in pounds.300
    1. M1 for the multiplier 0.85
    2. M1 for 255 ÷ 0.85
    3. A1 for £300
    Working: 100% − 15% = 85%, so the multiplier is 0.85. Then 255 ÷ 0.85 = £300.
    		3
    52(b) [2 marks] The shop paid £240 for the bike. What is the shop’s percentage profit on that cost when it sells the bike to Tia for £255? Give your answer like 12.5%.6.25% (also accept: 6.25)
    1. M1 for profit = 255 − 240 = 15, and 15 ÷ 240
    2. A1 for 6.25%
    Working: Profit = 255 − 240 = 15, and 15 ÷ 240 = 0.0625, and 0.0625 × 100 = 6.25%.
    		2
    53A price of £100 goes up by 20% and then goes down by 20%. Work out the final price. Answer in pounds.96
    1. M1 for 100 × 1.2 = 120
    2. A1 for 120 × 0.8 = 96
    Working: 100 × 1.2 = 120, then 120 × 0.8 = 96. The final price is £96. The 20% fall is taken from the bigger £120, so it takes off more (£24) than the rise added (£20).
    		2
    54A price rises by 25%. By what percentage must the new price fall to get back to the start? Give your answer like 12.5%. (Try starting with £100.)20% (also accept: 20)
    Working: £100 becomes £125. To return to £100 you take off £25. 25/125 = 0.2, so the fall is 20%. In multipliers: 1.25 × 0.8 = 1.
    55Without a calculator, find 4% of 75. Here is a trick: 4% of 75 is the same as 75% of 4.3
    Working: 75% of 4 = 3/4 × 4 = 3. The long way: 1% of 75 = 0.75, so 4% = 4 × 0.75 = 3.
    56Why is a% of b always the same as b% of a? For example, 8% of 25 = 25% of 8 = 2.Both are a × b ÷ 100, and multiplying works in either order
    Working: a% of b = a/100 × b = a × b ÷ 100. b% of a = b/100 × a = b × a ÷ 100. Multiplication can be done in either order, so they are equal.
    57A radio costs £96 with 20% VAT included. How many pounds of the £96 is VAT?16
    Working: 96 ÷ 1.2 = 80 is the price without VAT. VAT = 96 − 80 = £16. Check: 20% of 80 = 16. ✓
    58A boiler pressure valve costs £84.50 before VAT. VAT is 20%. How much does it cost including VAT, in pounds? Type it like 42.75.101.40
    Working: 20% VAT means multiply by 1.2. 84.50 × 1.2 = 101.40, so the price is £101.40. (Check: 20% of 84.50 is 16.90, and 84.50 + 16.90 = 101.40 ✓.)
    59A merchant gives a plumber 15% trade discount on a basin listed at £132. What does the plumber pay, in pounds? Type it like 42.75.112.20
    Working: 100% − 15% = 85%, so multiply by 0.85. 132 × 0.85 = 112.20, so the plumber pays £112.20. (Check: 15% of 132 is 19.80, and 132 − 19.80 = 112.20 ✓.)
    60A plumber buys a tap for £18.50. She adds a 30% markup (a percentage added to what she paid), and then 20% VAT is added to her selling price. How much does the customer pay, in pounds? Type it like 42.75.28.86
    1. M1 for the multiplier 1.3 (18.50 × 1.3)
    2. M1 for the multiplier 1.2 (× 1.2 for VAT)
    3. A1 for £28.86
    Working: Markup: 18.50 × 1.3 = £24.05. VAT: 24.05 × 1.2 = £28.86. The customer pays £28.86.
    		3
    61A plumber sells a shower valve for £126.50 after adding a 15% markup to what she paid. What did she pay, in pounds? Type it like 42.75.110
    Working: The selling price is 115% of what she paid, so what she paid = 126.50 ÷ 1.15 = 110. She paid £110. (Check: 110 × 1.15 = 126.50 ✓.)
    62A boiler service costs £95 plus VAT at 20%. A landlord gets 10% off the price before VAT. How much does the landlord pay in total, in pounds? Type it like 42.75.102.60
    1. M1 for the discount multiplier 0.9 (95 × 0.9)
    2. M1 for the VAT multiplier 1.2 (× 1.2)
    3. A1 for £102.60
    Working: Discount: 95 × 0.9 = £85.50. VAT: 85.50 × 1.2 = £102.60. The landlord pays £102.60.
    		3
    63The supply to a socket is 230 volts. Along a long cable the voltage drops by 5 volts. What is the voltage drop as a percentage of 230 volts? Give your answer to 2 decimal places, as a number without the % sign.2.17
    Working: The part is 5 V and the whole is 230 V. 5 ÷ 230 = 0.0217..., and × 100 gives 2.17... So the voltage drop is 2.17% (2 d.p.).
    64An electrician buys 40 m of cable at £1.85 per metre before VAT. VAT is 20%. How much does the cable cost including VAT, in pounds? Type it like 42.75.88.80
    Working: 40 × 1.85 = £74.00 before VAT. 20% VAT means multiply by 1.2: 74 × 1.2 = 88.80, so the cable costs £88.80. (Check: 20% of 74 is 14.80, and 74 + 14.80 = 88.80 ✓.)
    65A salon buys shampoo for £14.50 before VAT. VAT of 20% is added. What is the price including VAT? Type it like 12.50.17.40 (also accept: 17.4)
    Working: 20% of 14.50 is 2.90. 14.50 + 2.90 = £17.40. (Or 14.50 × 1.2 = 17.40 ✓.)
    66A salon has 80 bottles of shampoo in stock. After a busy week the stock is down 15%. How many bottles are left?68
    Working: 10% of 80 is 8 and 5% is 4, so 15% is 12. 80 − 12 = 68 bottles. (Or 85% of 80 = 0.85 × 80 = 68 ✓.)
    67A cut and colour usually costs £36. Students get 10% off. How much does a student pay? Type it like 12.50.32.40 (also accept: 32.4)
    Working: 10% of 36 is 3.60. 36 − 3.60 = £32.40. (Or 90% of 36: 0.9 × 36 = 32.40 ✓.)
    68Football boots cost £85. In a sale they are reduced by 30%. Club members then get a further 10% off the sale price. How much does a club member pay? Type it like 12.50.53.55
    1. M1 for 85 × 0.7 (= 59.50), or 85 − 25.50
    2. M1 for 59.50 × 0.9, or taking 10% off the sale price
    3. A1 for 53.55
    Working: Sale price: 85 × 0.7 = £59.50. Club member price: 59.50 × 0.9 = £53.55.
    		3
    69A salon raises the price of a cut from £28 to £31.50. Work out the percentage increase. Type the number only, like 7.5.12.5
    1. M1 for 31.50 − 28 = 3.50
    2. M1 for 3.50 ÷ 28 (= 0.125)
    3. A1 for 12.5
    Working: Increase: 31.50 − 28 = 3.50. Percentage increase = 3.50 ÷ 28 × 100 = 12.5%.
    		3
