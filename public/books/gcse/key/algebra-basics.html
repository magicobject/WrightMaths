    Algebra basics: answers

    Answers for Algebra basics
    No.QuestionAnswerMarks
    1A traditional kitchen scale balances. On the left pan there is a mystery weight and a 3 g weight. On the right pan there is a 10 g weight. How heavy is the mystery weight, in grams? [Figure: Kitchen scale with a mystery weight]7
    Working: Mystery weight + 3 = 10, so the mystery weight is 7 g. You have just solved x + 3 = 10 on a scale.
    2Last time: work out 3 + 4 × 2.11
    Working: 4 × 2 = 8, then 3 + 8 = 11.
    3Work out −2 + 5.3
    Working: −2 + 5 = 3.
    4Work out 2 × (3 + 4).14
    Working: 3 + 4 = 7, then 2 × 7 = 14.
    5If n = 4, what is n + 7? [Figure: Function machine: add 7]11
    Working: 4 + 7 = 11.
    6If n = 5, what is 3n?15
    Working: 3 × 5 = 15.
    7If n = 10, what is n/2?5
    Working: 10 ÷ 2 = 5.
    8Which expression means “5 less than n”?n − 5
    Working: n − 5. (If n = 8 then 5 less than 8 is 3: 8 − 5.)
    9Which expression means “double n, then add 3”?2n + 3
    Working: 2n + 3.
    10A tube of blue paint costs £b. Zara buys 3 tubes and a £2 brush. Write an expression for the total cost in pounds. (Type it like 3b+2.)3b+2 (also accept: 2+3b)
    Working: 3 tubes cost 3b pounds and the brush costs 2 pounds, so the total is 3b + 2.
    11Work out 2x + 3 when x = 4. [Figure: Function machine: 2x + 3]11
    Working: 8 + 3 = 11.
    12Work out 5a − 2 when a = 3. [Figure: Function machine: 5a − 2]13
    Working: 15 − 2 = 13.
    13Work out x² when x = −3. [Figure: Function machine: square]9
    Working: A negative times a negative is positive: (−3)² = 9.
    14Work out 3(y + 2) when y = 1.9
    Working: 3 × 3 = 9.
    15Work out a + b when a = 2 and b = −5.-3
    Working: 2 + (−5) = 2 − 5 = −3.
    16Work out 2x² when x = 3.18
    Working: 9 × 2 = 18.
    17A tube of blue paint costs £b. Zara buys 3 tubes and a £2 brush, so the cost is 3b + 2 pounds. Work out the cost when b = 4.14
    Working: 3 × 4 + 2 = 12 + 2 = 14, so the cost is £14.
    18A playlist has a 3-minute intro and then 4 songs of s minutes each. It lasts 4s + 3 minutes. Work out how long it lasts when s = 3.5.17
    Working: 4 × 3.5 + 3 = 14 + 3 = 17 minutes.
    19Simplify 3a + 2a. [Figure: Tiles for 3a and 2a]5a
    Working: 3a + 2a = 5a.
    20Simplify 7x − 3x.4x
    Working: 7x − 3x = 4x.
    21Simplify 4a + 3b + 2a. (Type it like 5p+2q.)6a+3b (also accept: 3b+6a)
    Working: 4a + 2a = 6a, and 3b stays: 6a + 3b.
    22Simplify 5x + 2 − x + 4. (Type it like 3k-7.)4x+6 (also accept: 6+4x)
    Working: 5x − x = 4x and 2 + 4 = 6: 4x + 6.
    23Write a × a as simply as possible. (Type it like m^2.)a^2 (also accept: a²)
    Working: a × a = a².
    24Write 3 × a × b as simply as possible. (Type it like 4rs.)3ab (also accept: 3ba)
    Working: 3 × a × b = 3ab.
    25Tiles are laid in a row: 3 pink tiles and 2 blue tiles, each a cm long, and 4 gold tiles, each 1 cm long. Write the total length in cm in its simplest form. (Type it like 7b+2.) [Figure: A row of tiles]5a+4 (also accept: 4+5a)
    Working: 3a + 2a = 5a, and the gold tiles add 4, so the length is 5a + 4 cm.
    26A sketchbook cover is 3 cm tall and (x + 4) cm wide. Expand 3(x + 4) to write its area. (Type it like 3x+12.) [Figure: Area model for 3(x + 4)]3x+12 (also accept: 12+3x)
    Working: 3x + 12.
    27The sketchbook cover's area is 3(x + 4) = 3x + 12 cm². Work out the area when x = 5, in cm².27
    Working: 3 × 5 + 12 = 27 cm². Check: 3 × (5 + 4) = 27.
    28Expand 2(a − 5). (Type it like 4m-6.)2a-10 (also accept: -10+2a)
    Working: 2a − 10.
    29Expand 5(2y + 1). (Type it like 3k+8.) [Figure: Area model for 5(2y + 1)]10y+5 (also accept: 5+10y)
    Working: 10y + 5.
    30Expand 4(m + 3) and then work out its value when m = 2. [Figure: Kitchen scale balance]20
    Working: 4(m + 3) = 4m + 12. When m = 2: 8 + 12 = 20. (Check: 4 × (2 + 3) = 20.)
    31x + 3 = 10. What value of x makes both sides equal?7
    Working: x = 7, because 7 + 3 = 10 and the scale balances.
    32x + 7 = 12. Does x = 5 make the scale balance? [Figure: Scale for x + 7 = 12]Yes: 5 + 7 = 12, so both sides are equal
    Working: 5 + 7 = 12 matches the right-hand side, so x = 5 balances it.
    332x = 14. What value of x makes both sides equal?7
    Working: 14 ÷ 2 = 7. Check: 2 × 7 = 14.
    34x − 4 = 9. What value of x makes both sides equal?13
    Working: x = 9 + 4 = 13. Check: 13 − 4 = 9.
    35You put 5 g more in the left pan. What keeps the scale balanced? [Figure: Forwards: x = 4 and 2x + 3 = unknown]Put 5 g more in the right pan
    Working: Add 5 g to the right pan as well: the same change to both sides keeps the balance.
    362x + 3 = 11. Find the value of x that makes both sides equal. [Figure: Scale for 2x + 3 = 11]4
    Working: 11 − 3 = 8 and 8 ÷ 2 = 4. Check: 2 × 4 + 3 = 11.
    373x + 12 = 3( ? ). Fill in the bracket. (Type it like n+6.)x+4 (also accept: 4+x)
    Working: 3x ÷ 3 = x and 12 ÷ 3 = 4, so 3(x + 4). Check by expanding: 3x + 12.
    386x + 9 = 3( ? ). Fill in the bracket. (Type it like 3n+5.)2x+3 (also accept: 3+2x)
    Working: 6x ÷ 3 = 2x and 9 ÷ 3 = 3, so 3(2x + 3). Check: 6x + 9. ✓
    39A rule: multiply n by 3, then add 1. When n = 1, 2, 3 the answers are 4, 7, 10. What is the answer when n = 10?31
    Working: 3 × 10 + 1 = 31.
    40A different rule is 3n + 2. When the answer is 17, what was n? [Figure: Inverse function machine]5
    Working: 17 − 2 = 15 and 15 ÷ 3 = 5. Check: 3 × 5 + 2 = 17.
    41Pat says: “3a + 2b = 5ab.” What went wrong?a and b are different letters, so they are not like terms. 3a + 2b cannot be simplified.
    Working: 3a + 2b stays as it is.
    42Quin says: “2(x + 3) = 2x + 3.” What went wrong?He multiplied only the x. The 2 multiplies everything in the bracket: 2x + 6.
    Working: 2(x + 3) = 2x + 6.
    43Rae says: “When x = 4, 3x = 34.” What went wrong?3x means 3 × x, not “3 and x”. 3 × 4 = 12.
    Working: 3 × 4 = 12.
    44Work out 5x − x² when x = 2.6
    Working: 10 − 4 = 6.
    45Simplify 8x − 3x + 2x. (Type it like 4k.)7x
    Working: 8x − 3x = 5x, and 5x + 2x = 7x.
    46Expand 4(x − 2). (Type it like 3m-5.)4x-8 (also accept: -8+4x)
    Working: 4x − 8.
    47Work out 3n + 10 when n = −2. [Figure: Function machine: 3n + 10]4
    Working: −6 + 10 = 4.
    48Which is NOT a like term with 3a: 5a, a² or 7a?a²
    Working: a² has a different letter part (a × a), so it is not like 3a.
    49(a) [2 marks] Work out the value of 2x² − 3x when x = −2.14
    1. M1 for working out both terms: 2 × 4 = 8 and −3 × (−2) = +6
    2. A1 for 8 + 6 = 14
    Working: 2 × 4 = 8 and −3 × (−2) = +6, so 8 + 6 = 14.
    		2
    50(b) [2 marks] Simplify 4x + 3 − x + 5. (Type it like 5p-2.)3x+8 (also accept: 8+3x)
    1. M1 for collecting the x terms (4x − x = 3x) or the numbers (3 + 5 = 8)
    2. A1 for 3x + 8
    Working: 4x − x = 3x and 3 + 5 = 8.
    		2
    51(c) [3 marks] Expand and simplify 2(x + 3) + 3(x − 1). (Type it like 5x+3.)5x+3 (also accept: 3+5x)
    1. M1 for expanding the first bracket: 2x + 6
    2. M1 for expanding the second bracket: 3x − 3
    3. A1 for collecting like terms to get 5x + 3
    Working: 2x + 6 + 3x − 3 = 5x + 3.
    		3
    52Work out x² + 3x when x = −2.-2
    Working: 4 + (−6) = −2.
    53Simplify 2(3x + 1) − 4x. (Type it like 6k-1.)2x+2 (also accept: 2+2x)
    Working: 6x + 2 − 4x = 2x + 2.
    54Expand (x + 1)(x + 4). (Type it like x^2+3x+2.) [Figure: Grid for (x + 1)(x + 4)]x^2+5x+4 (also accept: x²+5x+4, 4+5x+x^2)
    Working: x² + 4x + x + 4 = x² + 5x + 4. Check with x = 1: 2 × 5 = 10 and 1 + 5 + 4 = 10 ✓
    55Expand (x + 5)(x + 2). (Type it like x^2+6x+8.) [Figure: Grid for (x + 5)(x + 2)]x^2+7x+10 (also accept: x²+7x+10, 10+7x+x^2)
    Working: x² + 2x + 5x + 10 = x² + 7x + 10. Check with x = 1: 6 × 3 = 18 and 1 + 7 + 10 = 18 ✓
    56Expand (x + 3)(x − 1). (Type it like x^2-x-6.)x^2+2x-3 (also accept: x²+2x-3, -3+2x+x^2)
    Working: x² − x + 3x − 3 = x² + 2x − 3. Check with x = 1: 4 × 0 = 0 and 1 + 2 − 3 = 0 ✓
    57A plumber works out a quote with C = 45 + 38h, where C is the cost in pounds, 45 is the call-out fee (a fixed charge for coming to the job) and h is the number of hours. Find C when h = 3.5. Give your answer in pounds.178
    Working: C = 45 + 38 × 3.5 = 45 + 133 = 178. The cost is £178.
    58A quote is C = 60 + 42h + m, where h is the hours of labour and m is the cost of parts in pounds. Find C when h = 2.5 and m = 86.40. Type it like 42.75.251.40
    Working: C = 60 + 42 × 2.5 + 86.40 = 60 + 105 + 86.40 = 251.40. The quote is £251.40.
    59The number of 0.5 m square tiles needed for a floor is n = 4lw, where l is the length and w is the width of the floor, both in metres. Find n when l = 3.5 and w = 2.5. [Figure: A floor covered in square tiles]35
    Working: n = 4 × 3.5 × 2.5 = 4 × 8.75 = 35 tiles. (Check with the figure: 7 tiles along and 5 across, and 7 × 5 = 35 ✓.)
    60A quote with VAT is C = 1.2(45 + 38h + m), where h is the hours of labour and m is the cost of parts in pounds. Find C when h = 2 and m = 54.50. Type it like 42.75.210.60
    1. M1 for substituting h = 2 and m = 54.50 and working out 38 × 2 = 76
    2. M1 for the bracket 45 + 76 + 54.50 = 175.50
    3. M1 for multiplying by 1.2 (× 1.2 for VAT)
    4. A1 for 210.60
    Working: Inside the bracket: 45 + 38 × 2 + 54.50 = 45 + 76 + 54.50 = 175.50. C = 1.2 × 175.50 = 210.60. The customer pays £210.60.
    		3
    61The volume of water in a round pipe is V = 3.14r^2h, where r is the radius and h is the length, both in cm. Find V when r = 2 and h = 10, in cm^3.125.6
    Working: V = 3.14 × 2^2 × 10 = 3.14 × 4 × 10 = 125.6 cm^3.
