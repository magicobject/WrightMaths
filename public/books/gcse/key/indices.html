1Work out 5^2.25
Working: 5^2 = 5 × 5 = 25.
2What is the square root of 64?8
Working: 8 × 8 = 64, so the square root of 64 is 8.
3Work out 2 + 3 × 4^2, using the BIDMAS order.50
Working: 4^2 = 16. Then 3 × 16 = 48. Then 2 + 48 = 50.
4Work out (−2) × (−5).10
Working: Two negatives multiply to a positive: (−2) × (−5) = 10.
5Which of these is the same as 2^4?2 × 2 × 2 × 2
Working: 2^4 means four 2s multiplied together: 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16.
6Work out 3^4.81
Working: 3 × 3 = 9, 9 × 3 = 27, 27 × 3 = 81. So 3^4 = 81.
7A square patio is made of square slabs, laid in the pattern in the picture. How many slabs are there altogether? [Figure: A square patio of slabs]36
Working: 6 slabs in each row and 6 rows: 6 × 6 = 6^2 = 36 slabs.
8Write 10^5 as an ordinary number, like 1000.100000
Working: 10^5 = 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 = 100000 (a 1 followed by five zeros).
9Work out (−2)^3.-8
Working: (−2) × (−2) = 4, then 4 × (−2) = −8. So (−2)^3 = −8.
10On paper, −5^2 has no brackets, so only the 5 is squared and the minus sign stays outside. Work out −5^2.-25
Working: 5^2 = 25, and the minus stays in front: −5^2 = −25.

Working: After 6 hours the cells have doubled six times: 2^6 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 64 cells.

29Work out 9^0.1
Working: Any number (except 0) to the power 0 is 1. So 9^0 = 1.

44Write 0.0035 in standard form. Type it like 3.2x10^-5.3.5x10^-3 (also accept: 3.5*10^-3)

Working: The 1 is in the 10th place after the point, so the point moves 10 places to the right. 0.0000000001 = 1 × 10^−10.

56Use your calculator (the EXP or ×10^x key) to work out (2.4 × 10^6) × (3.5 × 10^2). Type it like 3.2x10^5.8.4x10^8 (also accept: 8.4*10^8)

  3. C1 for 7 × 2^n, stated as required
Working: 2^n+3 = 2^n × 2^3 = 8 × 2^n. 8 × 2^n − 2^n = (8 − 1) × 2^n = 7 × 2^n, as required.
