    Repeated percentage change: answers

    49 questions, 3 of them marked (10 marks in all). Question numbers match the page. Use your browser's Print for a copy.

    Open the questions Back to the teacher page
    Answers for Repeated percentage change
    No.QuestionAnswerMarks
    1Increase £200 by 5%. Answer in pounds.210
    Working: 1.05 × 200 = £210.
    2What multiplier increases an amount by 12%? Give it as a decimal.1.12
    Working: 100% + 12% = 112% = 1.12.
    3After a 20% discount a coat costs £48. What was the original price? Answer in pounds.60
    Working: 100% − 20% = 80% = 0.8. 48 ÷ 0.8 = £60.
    4A price rises from £40 to £46. What is the percentage increase? Give your answer like 12%. [Figure: Three years of the same multiplier]15% (also accept: 15)
    Working: Change = 46 − 40 = 6. 6 ÷ 40 = 0.15, and 0.15 × 100 = 15%.
    5£1000 grows by 5% each year. Which calculation gives the amount after 3 years?1000 × 1.05^3
    Working: The multiplier is 1.05 and it is used 3 times, so 1000 × 1.05^3.
    6£1000 is invested at 5% compound interest for 2 years. How much is it worth? Give your answer in pounds, like 4.50. [Figure: Two years at 5 percent]1102.5 (also accept: 1102.50)
    Working: 1000 × 1.05^2 = 1000 × 1.1025 = 1102.5, so £1102.50.
    7£800 is invested at 3% compound interest for 3 years. How much is it worth? Give your answer in pounds to the nearest penny, like 123.45.874.18
    Working: 1.03^3 = 1.092727. 800 × 1.092727 = 874.1816, so £874.18 to the nearest penny.
    8Zara invests £500 at 4% compound interest for 2 years. How much interest does she earn altogether? Give your answer in pounds, like 4.50.40.8 (also accept: 40.80)
    Working: 500 × 1.04^2 = 500 × 1.0816 = 540.8. Interest = 540.8 − 500 = 40.8, so £40.80.
    9A town of 12000 people grows by 2% each year. How many people live there after 5 years? Give your answer to the nearest whole number.13249
    Working: 1.02^5 = 1.10408… and 12000 × 1.10408… = 13248.97…, which is 13249 to the nearest whole number.
    10A game starts with 5000 players. The number of players grows by 10% every week. How many players are there after 3 weeks?6655 (also accept: 6,655)
    Working: 1.1^3 = 1.331. 5000 × 1.331 = 6655 players.
    11£2000 is saved at 5% for 2 years. How many pounds more interest does compound interest give than simple interest? Give your answer in pounds.5
    Working: Simple: 2 × £100 = £200. Compound: 2000 × 1.1025 = 2205, so interest £205. Difference: 205 − 200 = £5.
    12A phone’s battery life goes from 250 minutes to 290 minutes. What is the percentage increase? Give your answer like 12.5%.16% (also accept: 16)
    Working: Change = 290 − 250 = 40. 40 ÷ 250 = 0.16, and 0.16 × 100 = 16%.
    13A coat is reduced from £400 to £340. What is the percentage decrease? Give your answer like 12%.15% (also accept: 15)
    Working: Change = 400 − 340 = 60. 60 ÷ 400 = 0.15, and 0.15 × 100 = 15%.
    14The population of a village goes from 2400 to 3000. What is the percentage increase? Give your answer like 12%.25% (also accept: 25)
    Working: Change = 3000 − 2400 = 600. 600 ÷ 2400 = 0.25, and 0.25 × 100 = 25%.
    15A rent rises by 10% and then, a year later, by another 20%. What is the overall percentage increase? Give your answer like 12%.32% (also accept: 32)
    Working: 1.1 × 1.2 = 1.32, which is 132% of the start. That is a 32% increase.
    16A price rises by 20% and then falls by 20%. What is the overall change?A 4% decrease
    Working: 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96. That is 96% of the start, so a 4% decrease.
    17Show that a price that rises by 10% each year for 2 years has risen by 21% overall. Type the overall percentage increase you find, like 12%.21% (also accept: 21)
    Working: 1.1^2 = 1.21, which is 121% of the start. That is a 21% increase. (Two lots of 10% would only make 20%.)
    18A price rises by 4% and then falls by 2%. What is the overall percentage change, to 2 decimal places? Give your answer like 1.25%. [Figure: Two years of 10 percent depreciation]1.92% (also accept: 1.92)
    Working: 1.04 × 0.98 = 1.0192. That is 101.92% of the start, an increase of 1.92%.
    19A £1000 scooter loses 10% of its value each year. What is it worth after 2 years? Answer in pounds. [Figure: A scooter for two years]810
    Working: 0.9^2 = 0.81. 1000 × 0.81 = £810.
    20What multiplier shows a loss of 8% each year? Give it as a decimal.0.92
    Working: 100% − 8% = 92% = 0.92.
    21A van is worth £5000. It loses 20% of its value each year. What is it worth after 2 years? Answer in pounds.3200 (also accept: 3,200)
    Working: 0.8^2 = 0.64. 5000 × 0.64 = £3200.
    22A laptop is worth £900. It loses 30% of its value in year 1, and then 10% of its value in year 2. What is it worth after 2 years? Answer in pounds.567
    Working: 900 × 0.7 × 0.9 = 567, so £567.
    23A guitar worth £450 depreciates by 6% each year. What is it worth after 4 years? Give your answer in pounds to the nearest penny, like 123.45.351.34
    Working: 0.94^4 = 0.78074896. 450 × 0.78074896 = 351.337…, so £351.34 to the nearest penny.
    24A £1000 tablet loses 10% of its value each year. After how many whole years is it first worth less than £700?4
    Working: After 1 year: £900. 2 years: £810. 3 years: £729. 4 years: £656.10. So it is first under £700 after 4 years.
    25A car loses 10% of its value each year. After 2 years, how much of its original value has it lost? [Figure: Forwards: 1000 and unknown]19%
    Working: £100 becomes £90 and then £81. It has lost £19, which is 19%. (0.9^2 = 0.81.)
    26After 2 years at 10% compound interest, an account holds £1815. How much was paid in? Answer in pounds. [Figure: Working back from the end]1500
    Working: 1.1^2 = 1.21. 1815 ÷ 1.21 = £1500.
    27A van is worth £6400 after 2 years of 20% depreciation each year. What was its value when new? Answer in pounds.10000 (also accept: 10,000)
    Working: 0.8^2 = 0.64. 6400 ÷ 0.64 = £10000.
    28After 3 years of 10% growth each year, a town has 13310 people. How many people lived there at the start?10000 (also accept: 10,000)
    Working: 1.1^3 = 1.331. 13310 ÷ 1.331 = 10000 people.
    29£800 grows to £882 in 2 years at the same compound rate each year. What is the yearly rate? Give your answer like 3%.5% (also accept: 5)
    Working: 882 ÷ 800 = 1.1025. √1.1025 = 1.05, so the yearly rate is 5%.
    30A boat is worth £10000 and, 2 years later, £8100. It lost the same percentage of its value each year. What is that percentage? Give your answer like 3%.10% (also accept: 10)
    Working: 8100 ÷ 10000 = 0.81. √0.81 = 0.9. The yearly multiplier is 0.9, so it loses 10% each year.
    31£3000 grows to £3993 in 3 years at the same compound rate each year. What is the yearly rate? Give your answer like 3%.10% (also accept: 10)
    Working: 3993 ÷ 3000 = 1.331. The cube root of 1.331 is 1.1, so the rate is 10% a year. Check: 1.1^3 = 1.331. ✓
    32£1000 is invested at 8% compound interest a year. After how many whole years is it first worth more than £1500?6
    Working: Year 5: £1469.33. Year 6: 1000 × 1.08^6 = £1586.87. So it is first over £1500 after 6 years.
    33An account paid 4% compound interest each year for 3 years and now holds a known amount. How do you find the amount paid in?Divide by 1.04^3
    Working: Each year multiplies by 1.04, so 3 years multiplies by 1.04^3. To go back, divide by 1.04^3.
    34Zoe says: £2000 at 5% compound interest for 3 years is 2000 × 1.15 = £2300. What went wrong?She added 5% three times instead of multiplying.
    Working: 1.05^3 = 1.157625, not 1.15. 2000 × 1.157625 = £2315.25. Compound growth multiplies each year; it does not add 5% to the same starting amount.
    35Leo says: a van loses 20% of its value in each of two years, so it has lost 40% of its value. What went wrong?He added the 20%s, but the second is of a smaller amount.
    Working: £100 → £80 → £64. The van has lost £36, which is 36%. In multipliers: 0.8^2 = 0.64, so 64% is left.
    36Increase £250 by 12%. Answer in pounds.280
    Working: 1.12 × 250 = £280.
    37£400 is invested at 3% compound interest for 2 years. What is it worth? Give your answer in pounds, like 123.45.424.36
    Working: 1.03^2 = 1.0609. 400 × 1.0609 = £424.36.
    38A jacket costs £72 in a 20% sale. What was the price before the sale? Answer in pounds.90
    Working: 72 ÷ 0.8 = £90.
    39A price goes from £80 to £68. What is the percentage decrease? Give your answer like 12%.15% (also accept: 15)
    Working: Change = 80 − 68 = 12. 12 ÷ 80 = 0.15, and 0.15 × 100 = 15%.
    40Write 0.045 as a percentage. Give your answer like 12.5%.4.5% (also accept: 4.5)
    Working: 0.045 × 100 = 4.5, so 0.045 = 4.5%.
    41A car costs £15000 new. It loses 18% of its value in the first year. What is it worth after the first year? Answer in pounds.12300 (also accept: 12,300)
    Working: 100% − 18% = 82% = 0.82. 0.82 × 15000 = £12300.
    42(a) [3 marks] Work out the value of Dev’s investment after 3 years. Give your answer in pounds to the nearest penny, like 123.45.6461.34
    1. M1 for the multiplier 100% + 2.5% = 1.025
    2. M1 for 6000 × 1.025^3
    3. A1 for £6461.34
    Working: The multiplier is 100% + 2.5% = 1.025. Then 6000 × 1.025^3 = 6461.34375, which is £6461.34 to the nearest penny.
    		3
    43(b) [1 mark] How much interest has Dev earned in the 3 years? Give your answer in pounds, like 123.45.461.34
    Working: 6461.34 − 6000 = £461.34.
    44Work out the value of the car after 3 years. Give your answer in pounds, like 123.45.11151.36 (also accept: 11,151.36)
    1. M1 for the multipliers: 0.8 for year 1 and 0.88 for later years
    2. M1 for 18000 × 0.8 = 14400
    3. M1 for 14400 × 0.88^2
    4. A1 for £11151.36
    Working: Year 1 multiplier 0.8; later years 0.88. 18000 × 0.8 = 14400. Then 14400 × 0.88^2 = £11151.36.
    		4
    45A price rises by 5% and then falls by 10%. It is now £113.40. Work out the original price. Answer in pounds.120
    1. M1 for the overall multiplier 1.05 × 0.9 = 0.945
    2. M1 for 113.40 ÷ 0.945
    3. A1 for £120
    Working: The multipliers are 1.05 and 0.9, so overall 1.05 × 0.9 = 0.945. Then 113.40 ÷ 0.945 = £120. Check: 120 × 1.05 = 126 and 126 × 0.9 = 113.40. ✓
    		3
    46A price rises by 10%. By what percentage must it then fall to return to the original? Give your answer to 1 decimal place, like 12.5%.9.1% (also accept: 9.1)
    Working: 1 ÷ 1.1 = 0.90909…, so the multiplier is about 0.909. That is a fall of about 9.1%. Check: 1.1 × 0.909… = 1.
    47Account A pays 6% simple interest a year on £1000. Account B pays 5% compound interest a year on £1000. After how many whole years is account B first worth more than account A?9
    Working: After 8 years: A = £1480 and B = 1000 × 1.05^8 = £1477.46. A is still ahead. After 9 years: A = £1540 and B = £1551.33. So B first wins after 9 years. Compound growth starts slower but grows faster.
    48A price p rises by x% and then falls by x%. Which expression gives the final price?p × (1 + x/100) × (1 − x/100)
    Working: Rise: × (1 + x/100). Fall: × (1 − x/100). Together: p × (1 + x/100) × (1 − x/100). This equals p × (1 − x^2/10000), which is a little less than p whenever x is above 0.
    49After a price rises by x% and then falls by x%, the overall multiplier is 1 − x^2/10000. Use x = 30. What is the overall multiplier? Give it as a decimal.0.91
    Working: 30^2 = 900 and 900 ÷ 10000 = 0.09. So the multiplier is 1 − 0.09 = 0.91. Check: 1.3 × 0.7 = 0.91. ✓ The price ends 9% lower.
