1What fraction of the pizza has gone? [Figure: Pizza cut into 8 slices]5/8
Working: 5 slices out of 8 is 5/8. The slice size did not change, so the bottom stays 8.
2Work out 7 × 8.56
Working: 7 × 8 = 56.
3What is the highest common factor (the biggest number that goes into both) of 12 and 18?6
Working: Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18. The biggest in both lists is 6.
4What is the lowest common multiple (the smallest number in both times tables) of 4 and 6?12
Working: Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12. Multiples of 6: 6, 12. First common: 12.
5What is the denominator of 5/8?8
Working: The denominator is 8: the whole is cut into eighths.
6Fill the gap: 2/3 = (?)/12. What number goes where the ? is? [Figure: Two bars of the same length]8
Working: 3 × 4 = 12 so 2 × 4 = 8. 2/3 = 8/12.
7Simplify 12/18 fully. Give your answer as a fraction.2/3
Working: Divide top and bottom by 6: 2/3.
8Which is bigger: 3/5 or 4/7? [Figure: Comparing 3/5 and 4/7]3/5
Working: 3/5 = 21/35 and 4/7 = 20/35. So 3/5 is bigger.
9Work out 1/5 + 3/5.4/5
Working: 1 + 3 = 4 fifths: 4/5.
10Work out 1/2 + 1/3. [Figure: Halves, thirds and sixths]5/6
Working: 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.
11Work out 3/4 − 1/6. [Figure: Quarters, sixths and twelfths]7/12
Working: 9/12 − 2/12 = 7/12.
12Work out 2/3 + 3/4. Either an improper fraction or a mixed number is fine. [Figure: How many halves fit in three quarters]17/12 (also accept: 1 5/12)
Working: 8/12 + 9/12 = 17/12 = 1 5/12.
13Work out 2/3 × 3/5. Give your answer in its simplest form.2/5
Working: 6/15 = 2/5.
14Work out 3/4 ÷ 1/2. Give your answer in its simplest form, as a fraction or a mixed number (not a decimal).3/2 (also accept: 1 1/2)
Working: 3/4 × 2/1 = 6/4 = 3/2 = 1 1/2.
15Work out 2/3 ÷ 1/2. Give your answer in its simplest form, as a fraction or a mixed number (not a decimal).4/3 (also accept: 1 1/3)
Working: 2/3 × 2/1 = 4/3 = 1 1/3.
16Work out 3/4 × 8/9. Cancel first if you can. Give your answer in its simplest form.2/3
Working: Cancel 3 with 9 and 4 with 8: 1/1 × 2/3 = 2/3. Without cancelling: 24/36 = 2/3.
17A striker scored 3/8 of the team's 24 goals. How many goals did the striker score?9
Working: 24 ÷ 8 = 3, and 3 × 3 = 9 goals. The others scored 24 − 9 = 15.
18Work out 1 1/2 × 2/3.1
Working: 3/2 × 2/3 = 6/6 = 1.
19A playlist lasts 40 minutes. 3/5 of it is hip-hop. How many minutes of hip-hop is that? [Figure: Bar for 40 split into fifths]24
Working: 40 ÷ 5 = 8, so 3/5 of 40 = 3 × 8 = 24.
203/4 of a number is 18. What is the number? [Figure: Bar model: 3 quarters is 18]24
Working: 18 ÷ 3 = 6 is one quarter; 6 × 4 = 24.
21What fraction must you add to 2/5 to make 1? [Figure: Bar model: 2/5 plus what makes 1]3/5
Working: 5/5 − 2/5 = 3/5.
22Write 0.6 as a fraction in its simplest form.3/5
Working: 6/10 = 3/5. Check the other way: 3 ÷ 5 = 0.6.
23Write 0.375 as a fraction in its simplest form.3/8
Working: 375/1000 = 3/8. Check the other way: 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375.
24Write 1 ÷ 8 as a fraction.1/8
Working: 1 ÷ 8 = 1/8. Division and fractions are the same thing written two ways: 3/4 = 3 ÷ 4.
25Mia says: 1/3 + 1/4 = 2/7. What went wrong?She added the tops and the bottoms.
Working: She added the tops and the bottoms. The pieces must match first. Use twelfths: 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12.
26Sam says: 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/8. What went wrong?He multiplied them instead.
Working: He multiplied the fractions. Dividing asks how many quarters fit in a half: 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/2 × 4/1 = 2. There are 2 quarters in a half.
27Which of these is the biggest: 2/3, 3/5 or 5/8? [Figure: Comparing three fractions]2/3
Working: Over 120 they are 80, 72 and 75, so 2/3 is the biggest.
28A playlist has 24 songs. 3/4 of them are by UK artists. How many songs is that?18
Working: 24 ÷ 4 = 6, and 3 × 6 = 18.
29Ava trained on the basketball court for 5/6 of an hour. Mo trained for 1/3 of an hour. How much longer did Ava train? Give the answer in hours, in its simplest form.1/2
Working: 5/6 − 2/6 = 3/6 = 1/2.
30Write 2 1/4 as an improper fraction. [Figure: Two and a quarter]9/4
Working: 8/4 + 1/4 = 9/4.
31In a box of pencils, 2/5 are red and 1/4 are green. What fraction are neither red nor green? [Figure: Fifths, quarters and twentieths]7/20
Working: 8/20 + 5/20 = 13/20. So 20/20 − 13/20 = 7/20.
32A striker scored 3/8 of the team's goals this season. That is 15 goals. How many goals did the team score?40
Working: 1/8 = 5, so the whole is 8 × 5 = 40 goals.
33Work out 1/2 ÷ 3. Give your answer as a fraction in its simplest form. [Figure: A half split into 3 equal pieces]1/6
Working: 1/2 × 1/3 = 1/6.
34A florist has a ribbon 2 m long. She cuts off 3/4 m. Then she cuts a third of what is left for a bow. How many metres of ribbon are left now? Give your answer as a fraction in its simplest form.5/6
Working: 2 − 3/4 = 5/4 m. A third of that is 5/12 m. 5/4 − 5/12 = 15/12 − 5/12 = 10/12 = 5/6 m. Or: two thirds of 5/4 is 10/12 = 5/6.
35(a) [2 marks] How much does she spend altogether? Answer in pounds. [Figure: Jo spends fractions of £60]35
  1. M1 for 60 ÷ 3 = 20 and 60 ÷ 4 = 15
  2. A1 for 20 + 15 = 35
Working: 60 ÷ 3 = 20 and 60 ÷ 4 = 15. 20 + 15 = £35.

36(b) [2 marks] What fraction of Jo's money is left?5/12
  1. M1 for adding the fractions spent: 1/3 + 1/4 = 7/12
  2. A1 for 12/12 − 7/12 = 5/12
Working: Spent: 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12. Left: 12/12 − 7/12 = 5/12. Check: £25 left, and 25/60 = 5/12 ✓

37You know 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6. Without starting a fresh calculation, what is 5/6 − 1/2?1/3
Working: 5/6 − 1/2 = 1/3. Subtracting undoes adding. You can check it the other way: 1/3 + 1/2 = 5/6.
38Egyptians wrote fractions as sums of different unit fractions (fractions with 1 on the top). Two different unit fractions add to give 1/6. Their denominators add up to 25. What is the larger denominator?15
Working: Try each: 1/6 = 1/7 + 1/42 = 1/8 + 1/24 = 1/9 + 1/18 = 1/10 + 1/15. Only 10 + 15 makes 25, so the larger denominator is 15. Check: 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6.
39Work out 1/((1 × 2)) + 1/((2 × 3)) + 1/((3 × 4)) + … + 1/((9 × 10)). (Do not add all nine fractions the long way: look for a pattern.)9/10
Working: In general 1/n − 1/((n + 1)) = ((n + 1) − n)/(n(n + 1)) = 1/(n(n + 1)). So each term is a difference, and in 1 − 1/2 + 1/2 − 1/3 + … + 1/9 − 1/10 everything cancels except 1 − 1/10 = 9/10.
40A socket box needs a hole 3/4 of an inch wide. A drill bit is 5/8 of an inch wide. How much narrower than the hole is the bit? Type it as 1/8.1/8
Working: 3/4 = 6/8. 6/8 − 5/8 = 1/8, so the bit is 1/8 of an inch narrower. (Check: 5/8 + 1/8 = 6/8 = 3/4 ✓.)
41An electrician spends 1/3 of a day on a first fix and 1/4 of the day on testing. What fraction of the day is left? Type it as 5/12.5/12
Working: 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12 of the day used. The whole day is 12/12, so 12/12 − 7/12 = 5/12 is left.
42A 3 m length of conduit has 2/5 of it cut off. How many metres of conduit are left? Type it like 1.5.1.8
Working: 2/5 of 3 m = 3 ÷ 5 × 2 = 1.2 m is cut off. 3 − 1.2 = 1.8 m is left. (Check: 3/5 of 3 = 0.6 × 3 = 1.8 ✓.)
43An electrician’s bill is £240. The materials are 3/8 of the bill and the rest is labour. How much is the labour, in pounds? Type it like 42.150
Working: Labour is the rest: 8/8 − 3/8 = 5/8. One eighth of 240 is 30, so 5/8 is 5 × 30 = £150. (Check: materials are 3 × 30 = 90, and 90 + 150 = 240 ✓.)
44A box holds 240 cable clips. Sam takes 1/3 of the box and Ali takes 1/4 of the box. How many clips are left in the box?100
Working: Sam takes 240 ÷ 3 = 80. Ali takes 240 ÷ 4 = 60. Together they take 80 + 60 = 140, so 240 − 140 = 100 clips are left. (Check: they took 7/12 of the box, and 5/12 of 240 = 100 ✓.)
45A football match lasts 90 minutes. After 36 minutes, what fraction of the match has been played? Give it in its simplest form, like 3/4.2/5
Working: 36 out of 90 is 36/90. The HCF of 36 and 90 is 18. 36 ÷ 18 = 2 and 90 ÷ 18 = 5, so the fraction is 2/5.
46A squad has 24 players. 3/8 of them are defenders. How many defenders are there? [Figure: Bar model for the squad]9
Working: 1/8 of 24 is 24 ÷ 8 = 3. So 3/8 of 24 is 3 × 3 = 9 defenders. (The other 15 players and 9 defenders make 24 ✓.)
47A team scored 30 goals in a season. The striker scored 3/5 of them and the winger scored 1/6. What fraction of the goals did the rest of the team score? Give it in its simplest form, like 3/4.7/30
Working: 3/5 = 18/30 and 1/6 = 5/30. Together 18/30 + 5/30 = 23/30. The rest scored 1 − 23/30 = 7/30.
48A team played 40 matches in two seasons. They won 5/8 of them and drew 1/5 of them. How many matches did they lose?7
  1. M1 for 5/8 of 40 = 25
  2. M1 for 1/5 of 40 = 8
  3. A1 for 40 − 25 − 8 = 7
Working: Wins: 5/8 of 40 = 40 ÷ 8 × 5 = 25. Draws: 1/5 of 40 = 8. Losses: 40 − 25 − 8 = 7.
3
49A stadium has 1200 seats. 3/4 of the seats were sold, and 2/3 of the seats that were sold went to home fans. How many home fans were there?600
  1. M1 for 3/4 of 1200 = 900
  2. M1 for 2/3 of 900
  3. A1 for 600
Working: Seats sold: 3/4 of 1200 = 900. Home fans: 2/3 of 900 = 900 ÷ 3 × 2 = 600.
