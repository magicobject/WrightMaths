28A pen costs £x. Four pens and a £2 ruler cost £10 altogether. Form an equation and find x (in pounds). [Figure: Bar model for pens and a ruler]2
Working: 4x + 2 = 10, so 4x = 8 and x = 2. A pen costs £2.
29Tom is x years old. His sister is 3 years older. Their ages add up to 25. How old is Tom? [Figure: Bar model for the two ages]11
Working: 2x + 3 = 25, so 2x = 22 and x = 11. Tom is 11, his sister is 14 and 11 + 14 = 25 ✓

Working: The correct step is 3x = 20 − 5 = 15, so x = 5.

Working: 2x + 6 = 10, so 2x = 4 and x = 2.

36The sum of three consecutive whole numbers is 48. What is the smallest of them? [Figure: Bar model for three consecutive numbers]15

  2. M1 for collecting terms: 6x + 4 = 34, then 6x = 30
  3. A1 for x = 5
Working: 4x + 6 + 2x − 2 = 34, so 6x + 4 = 34 and 6x = 30. Then x = 5.

45Form an equation, with h for the number of hours, and solve it. How many hours did the plumber work? [Figure: Bar model for the plumber bill]4
  1. M1 for the equation 25 + 18h = 97
  2. M1 for 18h = 72
  3. A1 for h = 4
Working: 25 + 18h = 97, so 18h = 72 and h = 4. Check: 25 + 18 × 4 = 97 ✓

47Solve 5 − 2x = 3x − 10.3
Working: 5 = 5x − 10 &rArr; 15 = 5x &rArr; x = 3. Check: 5 − 6 = −1 and 9 − 10 = −1 ✓
48A plumber charges a £70 call-out fee plus £45 for every hour of work. A job costs £205. How many hours did the job take?3
Working: 70 + 45h = 205. Subtract 70: 45h = 135. Divide by 45: h = 3. The job took 3 hours. (Check: 70 + 45 × 3 = 70 + 135 = 205 ✓.)
49Plumber A charges a £60 call-out fee plus £48 an hour. Plumber B has no call-out fee and charges £64 an hour. After how many hours do they cost the same? Type it like 2.5.3.75
Working: Plumber A: 60 + 48h. Plumber B: 64h. Equal when 60 + 48h = 64h. Subtract 48h: 60 = 16h. Divide by 16: h = 3.75 hours. (Check: A is 60 + 180 = £240 and B is 64 × 3.75 = £240 ✓.)
50A quote is £60 call-out, plus 4.5 hours of labour at £x an hour, plus £86.40 of parts. The total is £362.40. Form an equation and find x, in pounds per hour.48
  1. M1 for the equation 60 + 4.5x + 86.40 = 362.40
  2. M1 for 4.5x = 362.40 − 60 − 86.40 = 216
  3. A1 for x = 216 ÷ 4.5 = 48
Working: Equation: 60 + 4.5x + 86.40 = 362.40. So 4.5x = 362.40 − 60 − 86.40 = 216. x = 216 ÷ 4.5 = 48. The rate is £48 an hour. (Check: 60 + 216 + 86.40 = 362.40 ✓.)
3
51A fitter buys 8 metres of copper pipe at £x a metre and pays £15 for delivery. The total is £65. Form an equation and find x, in pounds. Type it like 4.50.6.25
Working: 8x + 15 = 65. Subtract 15: 8x = 50. Divide by 8: x = 6.25. Each metre costs £6.25. (Check: 8 × 6.25 + 15 = 50 + 15 = 65 ✓.)
52A plumber cuts one third off a length of pipe, then cuts a further 25 cm off. 85 cm is left. How long was the pipe to start with, in centimetres? Call the starting length x.165
Working: x − x/3 − 25 = 85, so 2x/3 = 110. Multiply by 3: 2x = 330. Divide by 2: x = 165. The pipe was 165 cm. (Check: a third is 55, and 165 − 55 − 25 = 85 ✓.)
53An electrician buys cable at £2.40 a metre and pays £6 for delivery. The total is £102. How many metres of cable did she buy? Call the number of metres x.40
Working: 2.40x + 6 = 102. Subtract 6: 2.40x = 96. Divide by 2.40: x = 40. She bought 40 m. (Check: 2.40 × 40 + 6 = 96 + 6 = 102 ✓.)
54Ohm’s law says V = IR, where V is the voltage in volts, I is the current in amps and R is the resistance in ohms. A heater draws 10 A on a 230 V supply. Solve 230 = 10R to find R, in ohms.23
Working: 230 = 10R. Divide both sides by 10: R = 23. The resistance is 23 ohms. (Check: 10 × 23 = 230 ✓.)
