    Place value: answers

    41 questions, 1 of them marked (2 marks in all). Question numbers match the page. Use your browser's Print for a copy.

    Answers for Place value
    No.QuestionAnswerMarks
    1A gamer scores 4,082 points. What is the value of the digit 8 in that score? [Figure: Place value columns for 4,082]80
    Working: The 8 is in the tens column, so it is worth 8 tens, which is 80.
    2What is 1 more than 3,099?3100
    Working: 3,099 + 1 = 3,100. The 9s turn to 0s and carry into the next column.
    3Work out 10 × 10.100
    Working: 10 × 10 = 100.
    4Work out 10 × 100.1000
    Working: 10 × 100 = 1,000.
    5What is the value of the 7 in 3,725? [Figure: Place value columns for 3,725]700
    Working: The 7 is in the hundreds column: 700.
    6A song's stream count is 4 thousands, 0 hundreds, 3 tens and 5 units. Write the number of streams in digits. [Figure: Empty place value columns]4035
    Working: 4,000 + 0 + 30 + 5 = 4,035.
    7A fitness band shows “four thousand and thirty” steps. Write the number of steps in digits. [Figure: Empty place value columns]4030
    Working: 4,030. The zeros hold the hundreds and units columns open.
    8How many tens are there in 560? [Figure: Bar model: 560 made of tens]56
    Working: 560 ÷ 10 = 56. The 6 tens plus the 50 tens (500) make 56 tens.
    9Which is bigger: 9,099 or 9,101? [Figure: Place value columns for 9,099 and 9,101]9,101
    Working: Both have 9 thousands. Then hundreds: 1 beats 0. So 9,101 is bigger, even though its last digits look small.
    10What is the value of the 5 in 3.254? [Figure: Place value columns for 3.254]0.05
    Working: The 5 is in the hundredths column, so its value is 5 hundredths = 0.05.
    11Write 0.7 as a fraction.7/10
    Working: 0.7 = 7/10.
    12How many hundredths are there in 0.6? [Figure: Place value columns for 0.6]60
    Working: 0.6 = 0.60 = 60 hundredths.
    13Write 3 + 2/10 + 5/100 as a decimal.3.25
    Working: 3 + 0.2 + 0.05 = 3.25.
    14Which of these is the biggest: 0.4, 0.37 or 0.399?0.4
    Working: 0.400, 0.370 and 0.399: the biggest is 0.4.
    15Work out 3.4 × 100. [Figure: Place value columns for 3.4]340
    Working: 3.4 × 100 = 340.
    16A fan page has 5,600 likes shared evenly across 100 posts. Work out 5,600 ÷ 100, the likes per post. [Figure: Place value columns for 5,600]56
    Working: 5,600 ÷ 100 = 56.
    17A game gives you 0.07 coins for every click. You click 1,000 times. How many coins do you get?70
    Working: 0.07 × 1,000 = 70 coins.
    18Work out 2.5 ÷ 1,000. [Figure: Place value columns for 2.5]0.0025
    Working: 2.5 ÷ 1,000 = 0.0025.
    19Round 4,862 to the nearest 100. [Figure: Number line from 4,800 to 4,900]4900
    Working: 6 is 5 or more, so the 8 rounds up to 9: 4,900.
    20Round 6.749 to 1 decimal place. [Figure: Number line from 6.7 to 6.8]6.7
    Working: 4 is less than 5, so round down: 6.7.
    21A song has 15,650 streams. Round the streams to the nearest 1,000. [Figure: Number line from 15,000 to 16,000]16000
    Working: 6 rounds up, so 15,000 becomes 16,000.
    22Round 0.0527 to 1 significant figure.0.05
    Working: The first significant figure is 5. The next digit is 2, so round down: 0.05.
    23Round 3.996 to 2 decimal places. [Figure: Number line from 3.99 to 4.00]4 (also accept: 4.00)
    Working: 3.996 is between 3.99 and 4.00. The 6 after the hundredths rounds it up to 4.00 (3.99 + 0.01). Keep the zeros when the question asks for 2 d.p.
    24Round 7,350 to the nearest 100. [Figure: Number line from 7,300 to 7,400]7400
    Working: The digit after the hundreds is 5, which counts as “5 or more”, so round up: 7,400.
    25A number multiplied by 10 gives 340. What is the number?34
    Working: 340 ÷ 10 = 34.
    26A number divided by 100 gives 0.56. What is the number?56
    Working: 0.56 × 100 = 56.
    27What is the smallest whole number that rounds to 40 when rounded to the nearest 10? [Figure: Number line from 30 to 50]35
    Working: 35 rounds up to 40, but 34 rounds down to 30. So 35 is the smallest.
    28What is the largest whole number that rounds to 40 when rounded to the nearest 10? [Figure: Number line from 30 to 50]44
    Working: 44 rounds down to 40, but 45 rounds up to 50. So 44 is the largest.
    29A number rounded to 1 decimal place is 2.5. Which of these could be the original number?2.46
    Working: 2.46 has a 6 after the 4, so it rounds up to 2.5.
    30Ravi says: “3.45 is bigger than 3.5, because 45 is bigger than 5.” What went wrong?He compared 45 and 5 as if they were whole numbers.
    Working: Tenths: 4 against 5. The 5 is bigger, so 3.5 is the bigger number.
    31Zoe says: “6.07 × 10 = 6.70.” What went wrong?She moved only the 7. Every digit should move left.
    Working: 60.7. The 6 moves to the tens column, the 0 to the units and the 7 to the tenths.
    32Round 52,684 to the nearest 1,000. [Figure: Number line from 52,000 to 53,000]53000
    Working: 6 rounds up: 53,000.
    33What is the value of the 5 in 52,684? [Figure: Place value columns for 52,684]50000
    Working: The 5 is in the ten-thousands column: 50,000.
    34Work out 0.0048 × 1,000.4.8
    Working: 0.0048 × 1,000 = 4.8.
    35Which is the smallest of 0.5, 0.05, 0.505 and 0.55? Type the number.0.05
    Working: 0.500, 0.050, 0.505, 0.550. The smallest is 0.05.
    36Which of these is closest to 1,000?1,008
    Working: 950 is 50 away, 1,040 is 40 away, 1,008 is 8 away.
    37(a) [1 mark] Write the number of people to the nearest thousand.53000
    Working: 52,684 rounds up to 53,000.
    38(b) [1 mark] Write down the value of the digit 6 in 52,684. [Figure: Place value columns for 52,684]600
    Working: The 6 is worth 600.
    39(c) [2 marks] Estimate 48.7 × 19.8 by rounding each number to 1 significant figure.1000
    1. M1 for rounding both numbers to 1 significant figure: 50 and 20
    2. A1 for 50 × 20 = 1,000
    Working: 48.7 ≈ 50 and 19.8 ≈ 20, so 50 × 20 = 1,000. (The exact answer is 964.26, so the estimate is sensible.)

    40Round 0.04567 to 2 significant figures.0.046
    Working: 0.0456… ≈ 0.046 (the 6 rounds the 5 up).
    41A number rounds to 3,400 to the nearest 100. What is the largest whole number it could be? [Figure: Number line from 3,300 to 3,500]3449
    Working: 3,449 rounds down to 3,400, but 3,450 rounds up to 3,500. So 3,449.
