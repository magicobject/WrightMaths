    Speed, density and other compound measures: answers

    Answers for Speed, density and other compound measures
    No.QuestionAnswerMarks
    1A car travels 60 miles in 2 hours at a steady rate. How many miles does it travel in 1 hour?30
    Working: 60 ÷ 2 = 30, so the car travels 30 miles in 1 hour.
    2Make t the subject of d = s × t. Type your answer like m/k.d/s
    Working: d = s × t. Divide both sides by s: t = d ÷ s, which you type as d/s.
    3Change 2.5 hours into minutes.150
    Working: 2.5 × 60 = 150 minutes.
    43 books cost £12. How much do 5 books cost at the same price? Give your answer in pounds, like 12.50. [Figure: The distance, speed and time triangle]20.00 (also accept: 20)
    Working: 1 book costs 12 ÷ 3 = £4. 5 books cost 4 × 5 = 20, so £20.00.
    5A car travels 180 miles in 4 hours. What is its average speed in mph?45
    Working: S = D ÷ T = 180 ÷ 4 = 45 mph.
    6A runner goes at 12 m/s for 5 seconds. How far does she go? Answer in metres.60
    Working: D = S × T = 12 × 5 = 60 m.
    7Which formula for time is correct?time = distance ÷ speed
    Working: T = D ÷ S. For example 100 ÷ 50 = 2 hours.
    8Use the triangle. A coach travels 90 km at 45 km/h. How many hours does it take? [Figure: A triangle with two values and one unknown]2
    Working: Cover T: T = D ÷ S = 90 ÷ 45 = 2 hours.
    9A train travels at 75 mph for 3 hours. How far does it travel? Answer in miles.225
    Working: D = 75 × 3 = 225 miles.
    10A cyclist rides 36 km in 1 hour 30 minutes. What is her average speed in km/h?24
    Working: 1 hour 30 minutes = 1.5 hours. S = 36 ÷ 1.5 = 24 km/h.
    11A delivery van drives 45 miles at 60 mph. How many minutes does the drive take?45
    Working: T = 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75 hours. In minutes: 0.75 × 60 = 45 minutes.
    12A walker goes 6 km in 3 hours, then 4 km in 1 more hour. What is her average speed for the whole walk? Answer in km/h.2.5
    Working: Total distance = 6 + 4 = 10 km. Total time = 3 + 1 = 4 hours. Average speed = 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5 km/h.
    13A driver covers 100 miles in 2 hours, then 150 miles in 3 hours. What is the average speed for the whole journey? Answer in mph.50
    Working: Total distance = 250 miles. Total time = 5 hours. Average speed = 250 ÷ 5 = 50 mph.
    14A bus goes 30 miles at 20 mph, then 30 miles at 30 mph. What is its average speed for the whole journey? Answer in mph.24
    Working: First part: 30 ÷ 20 = 1.5 hours. Second part: 30 ÷ 30 = 1 hour. Total time = 2.5 hours. Average speed = 60 ÷ 2.5 = 24 mph.
    15How do you find the average speed of a whole journey?Total distance ÷ total time
    Working: Average speed = total distance ÷ total time. The speeds alone are not enough, because you spend different amounts of time at each.
    16A driver sets off at 09:15 and arrives at 11:45. The journey is 150 miles, including a 30-minute rest. What is the average speed for the whole journey? Answer in mph. [Figure: A journey on a distance-time graph]60
    Working: 09:15 to 11:45 is 2 hours 30 minutes = 2.5 hours. Average speed = 150 ÷ 2.5 = 60 mph.
    17The graph shows a journey. What is the speed in the first hour? Answer in km/h. [Figure: The journey graph]30
    Working: In the first hour the line goes up 30 km in 1 hour. Speed = 30 ÷ 1 = 30 km/h.
    18On the same graph, for how many hours was the traveller stopped? Answer in hours. [Figure: The journey graph]0.5
    Working: The flat section runs from 1 hour to 1.5 hours. 1.5 − 1 = 0.5 hours.
    19On the same graph, what was the speed on the last section, when the traveller came back? Give the speed as a positive number, in km/h. [Figure: The journey graph]60
    Working: The line falls 90 km in 4 − 2.5 = 1.5 hours. Speed = 90 ÷ 1.5 = 60 km/h.
    20On the same graph, how far did the traveller go altogether, there and back? Answer in km. [Figure: The journey graph]180
    Working: Out: 90 km. Back: 90 km. Total = 90 + 90 = 180 km.
    21On the same graph, what was the average speed for the whole journey from 0 to 4 hours? Answer in km/h. [Figure: The journey graph]45
    Working: Total distance travelled = 90 + 90 = 180 km. Total time = 4 hours. Average speed = 180 ÷ 4 = 45 km/h.
    22On a distance-time graph, which part shows the fastest travel?The steepest straight section
    Working: Speed is the gradient. The steepest section covers the most distance in the least time.
    23Change 36 km/h into m/s.10
    Working: 36 ÷ 3.6 = 10 m/s.
    24Change 20 m/s into km/h.72
    Working: 20 × 3.6 = 72 km/h.
    25A car travels at 108 km/h. What is its speed in m/s?30
    Working: 108 ÷ 3.6 = 30 m/s.
    26A sprinter runs 100 m in 10 seconds. What is her average speed in km/h? (Find the speed in m/s first.)36
    Working: Speed = 100 ÷ 10 = 10 m/s. In km/h: 10 × 3.6 = 36 km/h.
    27A cyclist rides at 18 km/h. How far does she go in 20 seconds? Answer in metres. (Change the speed to m/s first.)100
    Working: 18 ÷ 3.6 = 5 m/s. In 20 seconds: 5 × 20 = 100 m.
    28To change a speed from km/h to m/s, what do you do? [Figure: The mass, density and volume triangle]Divide by 3.6
    Working: 1000 ÷ 3600 = 1 ÷ 3.6, so km/h to m/s means dividing by 3.6.
    29A block of metal has a mass of 540 g and a volume of 200 cm³. What is its density? Answer in g/cm³.2.7
    Working: D = M ÷ V = 540 ÷ 200 = 2.7 g/cm³.
    30A metal has a density of 8 g/cm³. What is the mass of 25 cm³ of it? Answer in grams.200
    Working: M = D × V = 8 × 25 = 200 g.
    31A liquid has a density of 0.9 g/cm³. It has a mass of 720 g. What is its volume? Answer in cm³.800
    Working: V = M ÷ D = 720 ÷ 0.9 = 800 cm³.
    32A piece of wood has a mass of 150 g and a volume of 200 cm³. Water has a density of 1 g/cm³. What happens in water?It floats, because its density is 0.75 g/cm³
    Working: D = 150 ÷ 200 = 0.75 g/cm³. That is less than 1, so it floats.
    33A tub 10 cm long, 5 cm wide and 4 cm high is filled with sand of mass 300 g. What is the density of the sand? Answer in g/cm³.1.5
    Working: Volume = 10 × 5 × 4 = 200 cm³. Density = 300 ÷ 200 = 1.5 g/cm³.
    34A steel plate has a volume of 400 cm³ and a density of 7.8 g/cm³. What is its mass in kilograms, like 2.75?3.12
    Working: M = D × V = 7.8 × 400 = 3120 g. In kilograms: 3120 ÷ 1000 = 3.12 kg.
    35A block of concrete has a mass of 2.4 kg and a density of 2.4 g/cm³. What is its volume? Answer in cm³. (Change the mass to grams first.)1000
    Working: 2.4 kg = 2400 g. V = M ÷ D = 2400 ÷ 2.4 = 1000 cm³.
    36Higher A stone has a density of 2.5 g/cm³. What is its density in kg/m³?2500
    Working: 2.5 × 1000 = 2500 kg/m³.
    37Higher Steel has a density of 7800 kg/m³. What is this in g/cm³?7.8
    Working: 7800 ÷ 1000 = 7.8 g/cm³.
    38Higher A steel beam has a volume of 0.04 m³. Steel has a density of 7800 kg/m³. What is the mass of the beam? Answer in kg.312
    Working: M = D × V = 7800 × 0.04 = 312 kg.
    39Higher A material has a density of 0.8 g/cm³. What is its density in kg/m³? [Figure: The force, pressure and area triangle]800
    Working: 0.8 × 1000 = 800 kg/m³.
    40A force of 500 N acts on an area of 2 m². What is the pressure? Answer in N/m².250
    Working: P = F ÷ A = 500 ÷ 2 = 250 N/m².
    41A pressure of 40 N/m² acts on an area of 3 m². What is the force? Answer in N.120
    Working: F = P × A = 40 × 3 = 120 N.
    42A force of 900 N produces a pressure of 300 N/m². What is the area? Answer in m².3
    Working: A = F ÷ P = 900 ÷ 300 = 3 m².
    43A knife cuts well because of its thin edge. Why?The force acts on a tiny area, so pressure is large
    Working: The force from your hand does not change. A thin edge has a very small area, and pressure = force ÷ area, so the pressure is large.
    44A hydraulic pump pushes with a force of 1500 N on a piston with an area of 0.5 m². What is the pressure? Answer in N/m².3000
    Working: P = F ÷ A = 1500 ÷ 0.5 = 3000 N/m².
    45The pressure in a heating system is 150 000 N/m². It acts on a valve with an area of 0.002 m². What is the force on the valve? Answer in N.300
    Working: F = P × A = 150 000 × 0.002 = 300 N.
    46A joiner is paid £17.50 an hour. How much does she earn for 40 hours? Give your answer in pounds, like 480.00.700.00 (also accept: 700)
    Working: 17.50 × 40 = 700, so she earns £700.00.
    47A 25 kg bag of cement costs £7.50. What is the price per kilogram? Give your answer in pounds, like 0.45.0.30 (also accept: 0.3)
    Working: 7.50 ÷ 25 = 0.30, so the price is £0.30 per kg (30p per kg).
    48A 25 m reel of cable costs £32.50. A 50 m reel of the same cable costs £62.50. Which is the better buy?The 50 m reel, at £1.25 per metre
    Working: 25 m reel: 32.50 ÷ 25 = £1.30 per metre. 50 m reel: 62.50 ÷ 50 = £1.25 per metre. The 50 m reel is cheaper per metre.
    49An electrician is paid £22.50 an hour. How many hours does she need to work to earn £382.50?17
    Working: 382.50 ÷ 22.50 = 17 hours.
    50A tiler charges £28 per m². A floor has an area of 14.5 m². How much does the tiling cost? Give your answer in pounds, like 480.00. [Figure: The energy, power and time triangle]406.00 (also accept: 406)
    Working: 28 × 14.5 = 406, so the cost is £406.00.
    51A lamp uses 7200 joules of energy in 60 seconds. What is its power in watts?120
    Working: P = E ÷ T = 7200 ÷ 60 = 120 W.
    52A 2 kW heater runs for 3 hours. How many kWh does it use?6
    Working: Energy = 2 × 3 = 6 kWh.
    53A 500 W drill runs for 2 hours. How many kWh does it use?1
    Working: 500 W = 0.5 kW. Energy = 0.5 × 2 = 1 kWh.
    54Electricity costs 28p per kWh. How much do 8 kWh cost? Give your answer in pounds, like 3.50.2.24
    Working: 8 × 28 = 224p, which is £2.24.
    55A 3 kW heater runs for 12 minutes. How many kWh does it use?0.6
    Working: 12 minutes = 12 ÷ 60 = 0.2 hours. Energy = 3 × 0.2 = 0.6 kWh.
    56What does a kilowatt-hour (kWh) measure?Energy
    Working: Energy = power × time, so a kilowatt-hour measures energy.
    57An immersion heater uses 9 kWh of energy in 3 hours. What is its power in kW? [Figure: Forwards: D = 120, T = 2 and S = unknown]3
    Working: P = E ÷ T = 9 ÷ 3 = 3 kW.
    58A train travels at 90 km/h. How far does it travel in 40 minutes? Answer in km.60
    Working: 40 minutes = 2/3 hour. D = S × T = 90 × 2/3 = 60 km.
    59A tap fills 45 litres in 5 minutes at a steady rate. What is the rate in litres per minute?9
    Working: Rate = 45 ÷ 5 = 9 litres per minute.
    60The same tap fills litres at 9 litres per minute. How many minutes does it take to fill 117 litres?13
    Working: T = 117 ÷ 9 = 13 minutes.
    61A metal block has a mass of 1.8 kg and a density of 9 g/cm³. What is its volume in cm³? (Change the mass to grams first.)200
    Working: 1.8 kg = 1800 g. V = M ÷ D = 1800 ÷ 9 = 200 cm³.
    62A runner goes at 4 m/s. How long does she take to run 1.2 km? Answer in seconds.300
    Working: 1.2 km = 1200 m. T = 1200 ÷ 4 = 300 seconds.
    63A table pushes down with a force of 600 N. The pressure on the floor is 2400 N/m². What is the area of the table legs in contact with the floor? Answer in m².0.25
    Working: A = F ÷ P = 600 ÷ 2400 = 0.25 m².
    64Dan says: a bus goes 30 miles at 30 mph, then 30 miles at 60 mph. The average speed is (30 + 60) ÷ 2 = 45 mph. What went wrong?He averaged the speeds, not the distance and time
    Working: First part: 30 ÷ 30 = 1 hour. Second part: 30 ÷ 60 = 0.5 hours. Total = 60 miles in 1.5 hours, so 60 ÷ 1.5 = 40 mph, not 45 mph.
    65Eve says: a trip of 10 km takes 20 minutes, so the speed is 10 ÷ 20 = 0.5 km/h. What went wrong?She used minutes, but km/h needs the time in hours
    Working: 20 minutes = 1/3 hour. Speed = 10 ÷ 1/3 = 30 km/h.
    66Ida says: a force of 600 N acts on an area of 3 m², so the pressure is 600 × 3 = 1800 N/m². What went wrong?She multiplied. Pressure is force ÷ area, which is 200 N/m²
    Working: P = F ÷ A = 600 ÷ 3 = 200 N/m².
    67Which quantity is measured in N/m²?Pressure
    Working: N/m² is force divided by area, which is pressure.
    68A car travels 150 miles in 2.5 hours. What is its average speed in mph?60
    Working: 150 ÷ 2.5 = 60 mph.
    69A block of copper has a mass of 4480 g and a volume of 500 cm³. What is its density? Answer in g/cm³.8.96
    Working: 4480 ÷ 500 = 8.96 g/cm³.
    70A force of 360 N acts on an area of 0.12 m². What is the pressure? Answer in N/m².3000
    Working: P = F ÷ A = 360 ÷ 0.12 = 3000 N/m².
    71Cable costs £1.35 per metre. How much does 18 m cost? Give your answer in pounds, like 12.50.24.30 (also accept: 24.3)
    Working: 1.35 × 18 = 24.3, so the cost is £24.30.
    72A 1.5 kW kettle runs for 20 minutes. How many kWh does it use?0.5
    Working: 20 minutes = 1/3 hour. Energy = 1.5 × 1/3 = 0.5 kWh.
    73A pump moves water at 36 litres per minute. How long does it take to empty a 450-litre tank? Answer in minutes.12.5
    Working: T = 450 ÷ 36 = 12.5 minutes.
    74A 9 kW electric shower is used for 10 minutes. How many kWh of energy does it use?1.5
    Working: 10 minutes = 1/6 hour. Energy = 9 × 1/6 = 1.5 kWh.
    75An oak plank measures 200 cm by 20 cm by 4 cm and has a mass of 12 kg. What is the density of the oak? Answer in g/cm³.0.75
    Working: Volume = 200 × 20 × 4 = 16 000 cm³. Mass = 12 kg = 12 000 g. Density = 12 000 ÷ 16 000 = 0.75 g/cm³.
    76A concrete slab is 2 m long, 1.5 m wide and 0.1 m thick. Concrete has a density of 2400 kg/m³. What is the mass of the slab? Answer in kg.720
    Working: Volume = 2 × 1.5 × 0.1 = 0.3 m³. Mass = 2400 × 0.3 = 720 kg.
    77A delivery van leaves at 08:15 and arrives at 09:45 after driving 81 miles without stopping. What is its average speed in mph?54
    Working: 08:15 to 09:45 is 1 hour 30 minutes = 1.5 hours. Speed = 81 ÷ 1.5 = 54 mph.
    78An electrician is paid £24.50 an hour. How much does she earn for a job that takes 6.5 hours? Give your answer in pounds, like 120.50.159.25
    Working: 24.50 × 6.5 = 159.25, so she earns £159.25.
    79In a hydraulic jack, a force of 450 N acts on a small piston of area 0.003 m². The same pressure acts on a large piston of area 0.02 m². What force acts on the large piston? Answer in N.3000
    1. M1 for 450 ÷ 0.003 (= 150 000 N/m²)
    2. M1 for 150 000 × 0.02
    3. A1 for 3000
    Working: Pressure = 450 ÷ 0.003 = 150 000 N/m². Force on the large piston = 150 000 × 0.02 = 3000 N.

    80A cyclist rides 18 km in 45 minutes, then 12 km in 15 minutes. Work out her average speed for the whole ride. Answer in km/h.30
    1. M1 for total distance 18 + 12 = 30 km
    2. M1 for total time 45 + 15 = 60 minutes = 1 hour
    3. A1 for 30
    Working: Total distance = 18 + 12 = 30 km. Total time = 45 + 15 = 60 minutes = 1 hour. Average speed = 30 ÷ 1 = 30 km/h.
    		3
    81An aluminium bar is a cuboid measuring 15 cm by 4 cm by 3 cm. The density of aluminium is 2.7 g/cm³. Work out the mass of the bar. Answer in grams.486
    1. M1 for volume 15 × 4 × 3 (= 180 cm³)
    2. M1 for 180 × 2.7
    3. A1 for 486
    Working: Volume = 15 × 4 × 3 = 180 cm³. Mass = 2.7 × 180 = 486 g.
    		3
    82A car drives 90 km at 60 km/h. It then drives the same 90 km back at 45 km/h. Show that its average speed for the whole journey is 51.4 km/h to 1 decimal place.Written answer: see the mark scheme
    1. M1 for 90 ÷ 60 (= 1.5 hours)
    2. M1 for 90 ÷ 45 (= 2 hours)
    3. M1 for 180 ÷ 3.5
    4. C1 for 51.428… ≈ 51.4 stated
    Working: Out: 90 ÷ 60 = 1.5 hours. Back: 90 ÷ 45 = 2 hours. Total distance = 180 km. Total time = 3.5 hours. Average speed = 180 ÷ 3.5 = 51.428… ≈ 51.4 km/h to 1 d.p., as required.
    		4
    83A water tank rests on 4 feet. Each foot has an area of 25 cm². The tank pushes down on the ground with a force of 800 N. Work out the pressure on the ground. Answer in N/cm².8
    1. M1 for total area 4 × 25 = 100 cm²
    2. M1 for 800 ÷ 100
    3. A1 for 8
    Working: Total area = 4 × 25 = 100 cm². Pressure = 800 ÷ 100 = 8 N/cm².
    		3
    84A 3 kW heater runs for 2 hours 30 minutes. Electricity costs 28p per kWh. Work out the cost. Give your answer in pounds, like 4.50.2.10 (also accept: 2.1)
    1. M1 for 2 hours 30 minutes = 2.5 hours
    2. M1 for 3 × 2.5 (= 7.5 kWh)
    3. M1 for 7.5 × 28 (= 210p)
    4. A1 for £2.10
    Working: Time = 2.5 hours. Energy = 3 × 2.5 = 7.5 kWh. Cost = 7.5 × 28 = 210p = £2.10.
    		4
    85Higher A bar has a volume of 0.002 m³. It is made of a metal with a density of 7.8 g/cm³. Work out the mass of the bar. Answer in kg.15.6
    1. M1 for changing 7.8 g/cm³ to 7800 kg/m³
    2. M1 for 7800 × 0.002
    3. A1 for 15.6
    Working: 7.8 g/cm³ = 7800 kg/m³. Mass = 7800 × 0.002 = 15.6 kg.
    		3
    86A driver goes up a hill 1 mile long at 30 mph. How fast must she drive down the same mile to average 60 mph over the whole 2 miles?It is impossible, however fast she goes
    Working: For 2 miles at an average of 60 mph, the whole trip must take 2 ÷ 60 = 1/30 hour = 2 minutes. Going up 1 mile at 30 mph already takes 1 ÷ 30 = 1/30 hour = 2 minutes. There is no time left for the way down, so it is impossible.
    87Show that a speed of 1 m/s is the same as 3.6 km/h.Written answer: see the mark scheme
    Working: At 1 m/s, an object goes 1 m every second. In 1 hour there are 60 × 60 = 3600 seconds, so it goes 3600 m in 1 hour. 3600 m = 3.6 km. So 1 m/s = 3.6 km in 1 hour = 3.6 km/h.
    88Block A has a volume of 600 cm³ and a density of 2 g/cm³. Block B has a volume of 400 cm³ and a density of 7 g/cm³. They are stuck together. What is the density of the combined block? Answer in g/cm³.4
    Working: Mass A = 2 × 600 = 1200 g. Mass B = 7 × 400 = 2800 g. Total mass = 4000 g. Total volume = 600 + 400 = 1000 cm³. Density = 4000 ÷ 1000 = 4 g/cm³. (As with average speed, you use totals.)
