  2. 6 × 20 and 6 × 4, then add.
  • That is 24 + 6. The question has 6 packs of 24, so multiply.
  • That is only 6 × 20. Add 6 × 4 too.

  • That is the digit. Its value depends on its column.
  • That is the tens column. The 4 is one column further left.

The 4 is in the hundreds column: 400.

1. Adding and subtracting

The sum of two numbers is what you get by adding them. The difference is what you get by subtracting the smaller from the larger. Both written methods use columns. Line up the place values: units under units, tens under tens. Put the decimal points under each other.

Key idea: start at the right-hand column. Adding: if a column makes 10 or more, carry 1 into the next one. Subtracting: if the top digit is too small, exchange. Take one ten (or one hundred) from the next column and exchange it for ten ones. Some people call this borrowing. Keep digits in line. Most mistakes come from untidy columns.

Try it: in 38 + 27, what do you write in the units column, and what do you carry?

Worked example: add

Work out 4,578 + 3,964.

  1. Units: 8 + 4 = 12. Write 2, carry 1.
  2. Tens: 7 + 6 + 1 = 14. Write 4, carry 1.
  3. Hundreds: 5 + 9 + 1 = 15. Write 5, carry 1.
  4. Thousands: 4 + 3 + 1 = 8.
  5. 4,578 + 3,964 = 8,542.

Now you finish it: subtract

Work out 6,003 − 2,458.

  1. Units: 3 − 8 cannot be done.
  2. The tens and hundreds are 0, so exchange from the thousands: one thousand becomes ten hundreds, then ten tens, then ten units.
  3. 6,003 becomes 5 thousands, 9 hundreds, 9 tens and 13 units.
  4. 13 − 8 = 5, 9 − 5 = 4, 9 − 4 = 5, 5 − 2 = 3.
  5. So the answer is ___.

Easier way: count up from 2,458 to 6,003. 2,458 to 2,500 is 42. 2,500 to 6,000 is 3,500. 6,000 to 6,003 is 3. 42 + 3,500 + 3 = ___.

Check my answer

3,545 both ways. Check by adding: 3,545 + 2,458 = 6,003.

Check your knowledge: add and subtract

Work out 4,578 + 3,964.

  1. Units first: 8 + 4 = 12.
  • You forgot to add the carried 1 into the tens.
  • You forgot the carried 1 in the hundreds column.

8,542 (carry 1 three times).

Work out 6,003 − 2,458.

  1. Exchange from the thousands, or count up from 2,458 to 6,003.
  • You took the smaller digit from the bigger each time. Exchange instead.
  • You exchanged but did not reduce the thousands. 6 becomes 5 first.

6,003 − 2,458 = 3,545. Check: 3,545 + 2,458 = 6,003.

Work out 63 − 28.

  1. 3 − 8 cannot be done. Exchange one ten from the 6 tens for ten ones.
  2. Now units: 13 − 8. Then tens: 5 − 2.
  • You took 3 from 8. Exchange a ten for ten ones, so 13 − 8 = 5, then 5 − 2.

13 − 8 = 5 and 5 − 2 = 3, so 35. Check: 35 + 28 = 63.

A paint mix uses 12.7 litres of white paint and 8.45 litres of blue paint. How many litres is that altogether?

  1. Line up the points: 12.70 + 8.45.
  • You forgot the carry. 7 + 4 = 11: write 1, carry 1.
  • Line up the decimal points, not the right-hand ends.

12.70 + 8.45 = 21.15.

Work out 50 − 17.38.

  1. Write 50 as 50.00 and subtract.
  • Check by adding back: 33.62 + 17.38 = 51.

50.00 − 17.38 = 32.62. Check: 32.62 + 17.38 = 50.

What is the Difference between 83 and 127?

  1. Difference means subtract the smaller from the larger.
  • That is the sum. The difference is how far apart they are.

127 − 83 = 44.

2. Multiplying

The product of two numbers is the answer when you multiply them. For bigger numbers, split one number into easy pieces. This is partitioning. Multiply each piece, then add.

Key idea: 3.2 × 4: ignore the point, 32 × 4 = 128. 3.2 had one digit after the point, so the answer gets one: 12.8.

Try it: work out 24 × 3 by splitting 24 into 20 + 4.

Worked example

Work out 36 × 24.

  1. Split 24 into 20 + 4.
  2. 36 × 20 = 720 and 36 × 4 = 144.
  3. 720 + 144 = 864.

Worked example: decimals

Work out 0.3 × 0.4.

  1. Ignore the points: 3 × 4 = 12.
  2. 0.3 has one decimal place. 0.4 has one. So the answer has two.
  3. 0.3 × 0.4 = 0.12.

Sense check: multiplying by a number less than 1 makes things smaller. So the answer must be less than 0.3.

Check your knowledge: multiply

Six gamers each collect 47 coins. How many coins is that altogether? Work out 47 × 6.

  1. 40 × 6 and 7 × 6.
  • 7 × 6 = 42, not 12. Then 240 + 42.

240 + 42 = 282.

A track on a playlist lasts 124 seconds. You play it 5 times. How many seconds is that?

  1. Half of 124 × 10.
  • You left out 4 × 5 = 20. It is 500 + 100 + 20.

124 × 10 = 1,240, and half of that is 620.

Work out 36 × 24.

  1. Split 24 into 20 + 4.
  • That is only 36 × 4. You also need 36 × 20.
  • That is only 36 × 20. You also need 36 × 4.

720 + 144 = 864.

Work out 3.2 × 4.

  1. 32 × 4, then put the point back.
  • You lost the point. 3.2 has one digit after it.

32 × 4 = 128. 3.2 has one digit after the point, so 12.8.

Work out 0.3 × 0.4.

  1. 3 × 4 = 12. Two decimal places in total.
  • One digit after the point in each number makes two in the answer.
  • You lost the point. The answer needs two digits after it.

0.12.

3. Dividing

In 12 ÷ 4, the dividend is 12 (the number being divided). The divisor is 4 (the number you divide by). If a number does not divide exactly, what is left over is the remainder. Use short division and set out each step.

Key idea: division asks “how many groups of this fit in?” Or it asks you to share equally. To divide by a decimal, make the divisor whole. Multiply both numbers by 10 (or 100). Example: 3.6 ÷ 0.4 = 36 ÷ 4.

Try it: how many groups of 5 fit into 35?

Worked example

Work out 1,456 ÷ 7.

  1. 7 into 14 goes 2 times. Put the 2 above the 4.
  2. 7 into 5 does not go: write 0 and carry the 5 to make 56.
  3. 7 into 56 goes 8 times. Write 8.
  4. 1,456 ÷ 7 = 208.

Check by multiplying: 7 × 208 = 1,456. ✓

Check your knowledge: divide

A school raises £252 for its football club. The money is shared equally between 6 teams. How many pounds does each team get?

  1. 25 ÷ 6 = 4 remainder 1.
  • You missed the carry. 25 ÷ 6 = 4 remainder 1, then 12 ÷ 6.

252 ÷ 6 = 42. Check: 6 × 42 = 252.

Work out 1,456 ÷ 7.

  1. 14 ÷ 7 = 2, then 5, then 56.
  • 5 ÷ 7 does not go, so write a 0 in the answer.

208. Check: 7 × 208 = 1,456.

What is the remainder when 95 is divided by 4?

  1. 4 × 23 = 92.
  • That is how many whole 4s fit. The remainder is what is left over.

4 × 23 = 92 and 95 − 92 = 3.

Write 95 ÷ 4 as a decimal.

  1. 23 remainder 3: the 3 is three quarters.
  • A remainder of 3 out of 4 is 0.75, not 0.3.

95 ÷ 4 = 23.75.

Work out 3.6 ÷ 0.4.

  1. Multiply both numbers by 10: 36 ÷ 4.
  • You moved one number only. Multiply both by 10: 36 ÷ 4.

36 ÷ 4 = 9.

Flip it: every operation has a partner that undoes it

So far you have gone one way. Now run it backwards. Same idea, other direction.

Forwards

342 + 289 = 631

Add to build up.

Backwards

631 − 289 = 342

Subtract to undo it.

Key idea: add and subtract are inverse operations, and so are multiply and divide. Use this to check an answer by reversing it. Use it to find a missing number by undoing the steps.

Forwards

24 × 6 = 144

Six groups of 24.

Backwards

144 ÷ 6 = 24, and 144 ÷ 24 = 6

One multiplication gives you two divisions.

Check your knowledge: flip it

You know 342 + 289 = 631. What is 631 − 289?

  1. Subtracting undoes adding.
  • That is 631 + 289. Subtract to undo the addition.

631 − 289 = 342: you get back to where you started.

I think of a number, multiply it by 7 and get 91. What is my number?

  1. Undo × 7 with ÷ 7.
  • That is 91 × 7. Undo a multiplication by dividing.

91 ÷ 7 = 13. Check: 13 × 7 = 91.

46 × ? = 1,196. Find the missing number.

  1. Undo with division: 1,196 ÷ 46.
  • That is 46 × 1,196. Divide to find the missing factor.

1,196 ÷ 46 = 26. Check: 46 × 26 = 1,196.

? − 138 = 267. Find the missing number.

  1. The missing number is bigger. Undo − 138 with + 138.
  • That is 267 − 138. To undo a subtraction, add.

267 + 138 = 405. Check: 405 − 138 = 267.

? ÷ 8 = 15. Find the missing number.

  1. Undo ÷ 8 with × 8.
  • That is 15 ÷ 8. Undo a division by multiplying.

15 × 8 = 120.

You know 24 × 6 = 144. Write the answer to 144 ÷ 24.

  1. One multiplication gives two divisions.
  • You divided and got 24 back. Which factor is left?

144 ÷ 24 = 6, because 24 × 6 = 144.

4. Using it in real situations

First decide which operation to use. Words can give clues. “Altogether” often means add. “Change” (the money you get back) means subtract. “Shared equally” means divide. But clues can mislead: “each” can mean multiply or divide. Picture what is happening. Then make an estimate to check the size of your answer.

Key idea: if the answer counts whole things, a remainder usually means one more. 70 students, with 16 in each minibus (a small bus). You need 5 minibuses, not 4.375.

Try it: 25 people, 4 in each car. How many cars?

Check your knowledge: problems

  • 4 tickets.
  • Each ticket costs £8.50. (£ means pounds, the UK money.)
  • There is one £3 booking fee for the whole order.

What is the total cost in pounds?

  1. Four tickets first: 4 × 8.50.
  2. Then add the booking fee once.
  • That adds. There are four tickets at £8.50 each.
  • You charged £3 on every ticket. It is charged once.
  • You left out the booking fee. Add £3 once.

4 × 8.50 = 34, and 34 + 3 = £37.

156 pencils are shared equally between 12 children. How many pencils does each child get?

  1. Shared equally means divide.
  • That is 156 + 12. Sharing means dividing.
  • That is 156 − 12. Sharing equally means dividing.

156 ÷ 12 = 13. Check: 12 × 13 = 156.

70 students go on a trip. Each minibus (a small bus) holds 16 students. How many minibuses are needed?

  1. 70 ÷ 16 is between 4 and 5.
  2. You cannot have part of a minibus.
  • 4 minibuses hold 64. Six students would be left behind.
  • That is the exact answer. You cannot hire part of a minibus.

70 ÷ 16 = 4.375, so 4 minibuses would leave some students behind. You need 5.

A bag holds 1.5 kg of flour. How many 250 g portions can you make? (1 kg = 1,000 g.)

  1. Use grams first: 1.5 kg = 1,500 g.
  • That divides 1.5 by 250. Use the same unit: 1,500 ÷ 250.
  • That multiplies. How many 250 g portions fit? Divide.

1,500 ÷ 250 = 6.

Jo saves £12.50 each week. How many weeks does it take her to save at least £100?

  1. 100 ÷ 12.50 = 8 exactly.
  • 7 weeks gives only £87.50.

12.50 × 8 = 100, so 8 weeks.

Spot the mistake

Mia says: “6,003 − 2,458 = 4,455.” (She did each column by taking the smaller digit from the bigger.) What went wrong?

  • The smaller digit was taken from the bigger every time.
  • Nothing is wrong: 4,455 is the right answer.
  • She should have added the two numbers.
  1. Check by adding back: 4,455 + 2,458.

The correct answer is 3,545. Check: 3,545 + 2,458 = 6,003.

Tom says: “3.5 × 0.2 = 7.” What went wrong?

  • The decimal point was lost from the answer.
  • Nothing is wrong: 35 × 2 = 70, so 7 fits.
  • He should have divided instead of multiplying.
  1. Estimate: 3.5 is near 4. 0.2 is one fifth. A fifth of 4 is less than 1.

35 × 2 = 70. 3.5 and 0.2 have one digit after the point each, so the answer has two: 0.70 = 0.7. A fifth of 3.5 is 0.7.

Check your knowledge: mixed review

A game character has 52 health points, takes 17 damage and then heals 8. How many health points does it have now?

  1. Work from left to right.
  • You did 17 + 8 first. Go left to right.

52 − 17 = 35, and 35 + 8 = 43.

Ava has 3.2 m of ribbon and buys 0.85 m more. How many metres of ribbon does she have?

  1. 3.20 + 0.85.
  • The digits were not lined up by place. Line up the points: 3.20 + 0.85.

4.05.

Work out 125 × 8.

  1. Half of 125 × 16, or 100 × 8 + 25 × 8.
  • Check: 25 × 8 = 200, so 800 + 200.

800 + 200 = 1,000.

A music app has 432 songs. They are split into playlists of 12 songs each. How many playlists is that?

  1. 12 × 30 = 360, then how many more 12s?
  • That is only the first part. 432 − 360 = 72, and 72 ÷ 12 = 6 more.

12 × 36 = 432.

A box holds 12 eggs. How many boxes are needed for 100 eggs?

  1. 100 ÷ 12 = 8 remainder 4.
  • 8 boxes hold only 96 eggs.
  • You cannot buy part of a box.

8 boxes hold 96 eggs. 4 eggs are left over, so you need 9 boxes.

Exam style

  • An adult ticket costs £12.50.
  • A child ticket costs £7.25.
  • A group buys 2 adult tickets and 3 child tickets.

Write your working on paper, then type the answer.

(a) [3 marks] Work out the total cost, in pounds.

  1. Adults: 2 × 12.50. Children: 3 × 7.25.
  2. Add the two totals.
  • That is one adult and one child. There are 2 and 3.
  • That is only the children. Add the adults.

2 × 12.50 = 25 and 3 × 7.25 = 21.75. 25 + 21.75 = £46.75.

(b) [2 marks] They pay with a £50 note. How much change (the money they get back) do they get, in pounds?

  1. Change = 50 − the total.
  • That adds. Change is what is left of the £50.

50 − 46.75 = £3.25. Check: 46.75 + 3.25 = 50.

  • A coach has 52 seats.
  • 4 coaches are completely full.
  • A fifth coach has 37 people.

(c) [3 marks] How many people are there altogether?

  1. 4 full coaches: 4 × 52.
  2. Then add the 37.
  • That is 52 + 37. There are 4 full coaches.
  • That is only the 4 full coaches. Add the 37 as well.

4 × 52 = 208 and 208 + 37 = 245.

Going further

Use the digits 2, 3 and 4 once each to make a two-digit number multiplied by a one-digit number. What is the largest product you can make?

  1. There are only six ways to arrange 2, 3 and 4. Try them all and compare.
  • Putting the biggest digits together does not give the biggest product. Try all six.

43 × 2 = 86, 42 × 3 = 126, 32 × 4 = 128, 34 × 2 = 68, 24 × 3 = 72, 23 × 4 = 92. The largest is 32 × 4 = 128. Putting the biggest digit (4) on its own as the multiplier wins.

You know 4,872 ÷ 24 = 203. What is 203 × 24?

  1. Multiplying undoes dividing.
  • That is 203 + 24. Multiplying by 24 undoes dividing by 24.

203 × 24 = 4,872, the number you started with.

Why it works

  • Carrying and exchanging are just place value: ten units become one ten, and one ten becomes ten units.
  • Partitioning works because multiplication spreads over addition: 36 × 24 = 36 × 20 + 36 × 4.
  • Inverse operations undo each other, so every answer can be checked by running it backwards.

Common traps

  • Not lining up place values or decimal points.
  • Taking the smaller digit from the bigger in every column.
  • Losing the decimal point after multiplying.
  • Forgetting the zero in an answer such as 208.
  • Giving 4.375 minibuses when a whole number was needed.
